### Decisions
- **Jobber is the source of truth for day and tech; OptimoRoute is the optimizer** (Spencer). It may re-sequence within a day and hand times back — never reassign or move days. `jobber-to-optimo-sync.mjs` implements this; `grid-tech-realign.mjs` does the reverse and will fight it.
- **Arrival-window freeze removed as a blocker** (Spencer: "the route changes all the time and it is what it is"). Customers are not re-notified on re-times.
- **Cadence comes from what the tech found, not what they typed.** Quick Fix = always weekly for its 5-week series; finished + activity → Task (sales call), finished + quiet → nothing owed. TMCP = catch or ANY activity (L/A, M/A, H/A) → weekly; `N/A` with no catch → monthly.
- **Product is identified by line item, not `jobType`** — every Got Moles job is RECURRING, Quick Fix included.
- **Continuity is measured from 2026-08-03 forward, not from history.** Before the 3→4 route split Alias rode with Cory, 21% of visits carried two assignees, and Spencer Hill / Robert Norton were still on the board — "who served them last" is unanswerable before that date.
- **Did NOT run the 110-visit continuity restore.** Measured against the correct baseline (the hand-assigned week) next week is 260/268 = 97% correct, only 8 differ. The apparent "50% break" was an artifact, and restoring would have reversed Spencer's Aug 1 territory re-cut and given Cammeron a 44-stop Tuesday.

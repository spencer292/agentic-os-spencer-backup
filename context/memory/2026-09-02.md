### Decisions
- Metric definitions copied verbatim from `ninety-weekly-push.mjs` (45-day TMCP guard, archived-job exclusion, $25–400 max-line-item monthly rate, product regexes) so the dashboard and the Ninety board cannot disagree.
- **Projection is shape-based, not pace-based.** First build extrapolated daily invoicing pace and projected $411,753 for September on day 2, against real months of $78K / $99K / $109K. Got Moles bills in batches (2026-08-31 alone = $56,361, the two days before it = $0), so fraction-of-month-billed has almost nothing to do with fraction-of-month-elapsed. Now: already-billed + what an average month still has left at this point in its measured shape. Landed at $97,631. Measured shape at day 2 = **26.4% of a month's billing already done vs 6.7% of days elapsed** — billing is heavily front/back-loaded around the month boundary.
- Dashboard cannot call the APIs itself (credentials would sit in the file; both APIs refuse browser origins) — hence the baked-in-data + cron + .bat design.
- Cron moved 05:30 → 05:00 to avoid colliding with `ninety-scorecard-push` (also 05:30 Mondays); two heavy Jobber sweeps at once throttle each other.
- Comparison columns print their actual date ranges, because with a Sunday week start, week-to-date legitimately exceeds month-to-date in the first days of a month (80 calls WTD vs 44 MTD on 09-02) and reads as a bug otherwise.
- Chart palette is the validated data-viz default; all six checks pass light and dark. Do not swap hexes without re-running `validate_palette.js`.

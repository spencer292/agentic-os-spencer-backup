### Decisions
- **Gate at the HTTP transport, not the call-site.** All 117 scripts use global fetch and there are only two Jobber endpoints, so one import line per script covers everything and the coverage checker can prove it. A per-call-site guard would have needed 32 edits and been absent from the next script written.
- **Grants must expire.** An open-ended grant (`enabledUntil: null`) is refused outright. The failure mode being guarded is a grant made for one run and never revoked — that is how route-drift-check came to be rewriting the board unattended.
- **One narrow standing grant** for `arrival-window-sweep.mjs` (jobber `jobEdit` only) so the customer-facing safety net survives writes-off-by-default. Verified it can do jobEdit and nothing else.
- **Office pages derive from real visit history, not any map.** Measured: the v5 grid the cron was pinned to disagreed with reality on 64 of 118 zips, and on 24 of those named a day no truck runs (666 visits behind them) — including 98328 Eatonville, the exact zip from the Brian Honig "we're there Fridays" call.
- **Spec v2's defect D3 was wrong and was NOT applied as written.** `sr-516 crossesOwners: false` is correct from 2026-08-17: the handover table moves "North of SR-516" from Cory to Robert, and the south side was already Robert. Flipping it to true would have been a regression. The real defect was the stale `ownerFrom20260817` field claiming Tavis owned it — 2 of 6 instances contradicted the handovers, and nothing read the field. Fixed by deriving instead of storing.

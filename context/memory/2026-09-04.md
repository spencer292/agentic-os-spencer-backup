### Findings (short)
- Market is real and dense: WA has 4,000+ coffee shops, 57.3 per 100k (2nd in US behind Alaska); ~1,800 addressable independents in the Puget Sound corridor after stripping ~700 Starbucks and the franchise chains.
- Competition exists at three tiers: paid consultants (Seattle Barista Academy $1,050/day training, $1,200/day store eval, 20 min from the prospects), FREE roaster training bundled into every wholesale bean contract, and a productized $49/mo subscription (Coffee Shop Success' Essential Barista Training).
- Buyer is weak: 2.5–7% net margins, 50–74% five-year failure rate, wages up from $12–14 to $15–18/hr since 2020.
- The killer risk: in the PNW drive-thru market, sugar/fat/temperature mask espresso quality, so better coffee may not produce measurable revenue lift — no ROI story, no price ceiling.
- Pure day-rate consulting tops out around $60–75k net each. Not scalable.

### Decisions
- Recommended two scalable pivots instead of the stated idea: (A) sell training to regional ROASTERS as their outsourced training arm — one sale reaches 30–80 accounts and converts the "free roaster training" objection into the distribution channel; (B) training as door-opener, monthly espresso calibration + PM route as the business (~$200/mo/stand, 60 stands = $12k MRR) — structurally the same shape as Got Moles' route model, but needs a real espresso tech.
- Gave a 30-day, sub-$500 validation test with a pre-set decision rule (20 stands scored in person, 10 free sessions, 3 asked to pay $500, 3 roaster phone calls).

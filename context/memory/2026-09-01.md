### Decisions
- Archive, not delete: Jobber has no `clientDelete` mutation, only `clientArchive`/`clientUnarchive`. Spencer accepted archive as the practical equivalent.
- A Request does NOT count as real history — the CallRail integration files one per call. Only jobs, quotes, invoices and balance gate an archive.
- An empty `lastName` is not junk on its own: real records ("John Green", "1st Baptist Church") carry the whole name in `firstName`. Classify on the rendered name, not the field split.
- Batch A (204 records, nothing attached) executed and verified. 0 failures.

- Batch A escalated from archive to PERMANENT DELETE on Spencer's call ("no reason to keep the archived garbage"). 204/204 deleted via the UI, verified 0 survivors.
- The real complaint turned out to be a phone collision, not a naming annoyance: an inbound text shows the name of whichever client record holds the number, and a duplicate splits the thread. Merge (UI-only) is the fix, not archive or rename.

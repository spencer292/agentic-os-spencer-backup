### THE BIG ONE — manufacturing claims
Spencer: *"most of these products are made by subcontractors and on the website it says made in house."*

He was right. The plan's premise ("SY is a billet parts manufacturer hiding inside a reseller storefront") was inherited by the Phase 0 audit and by everything built on it. **I refused to invent warranty terms and lead times, then wrote manufacturing claims without applying the same test — because they came from the plan rather than from me. An inherited claim is still a shipped claim.**

First pass I overcorrected to cautious "design" language. Spencer pushed back — hiding subcontracting steals brand authority. He was right again. **Settled position:**
- **SYPerformance IS the manufacturer** — owns designs, drawings, specs; contracts production; inspects; warranties. Standard industry meaning, most respected brands in this space are identical. Use it: *we manufacture, we make, our parts, our spec*.
- **Never name a shop floor** — no *machined in our own shop*, *cut here*, *on our machines*. Checkable, and the only version that can be disproved.
- **Nothing on the site mentions subcontracting** and never did.
- Final: badge = `SYPerformance`, hero = "We make the drivetrain parts that break first", story = "We manufacture our own parts. We don't rebadge someone else's."

Rule written into `docs/ia.md` §4 AND `snippets/syp-badge.liquid` so it can't drift either way. Full record: `docs/checklist.md` §1b.

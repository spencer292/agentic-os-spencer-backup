### Decisions
- **Visit start times and windows are PLACEHOLDERS, not customer promises** (Spencer). Pinning on
  them held 28 visits off their grid day and made routine moves look like broken appointments. Both
  `push-week` and `optimize-week` corrected to pin only on real SETs.
- **Overtime = over 40h/WEEK; up to 2h/week approved; hard ceiling 42h.** Hours are measured first
  stop to last stop — commute is unpaid and not counted.
- **Territory is the default, overflow the exception.** Overflow moves ONE JOB at a time to whichever
  tech's route passes nearest, max 15 min detour, cascading if the neighbour is also over.
- **Day/tech stability is a preference, not a hard rule** (relaxed mid-session).
- **Level days WITHIN a tech, not just the week.** A 9h day next to a 5h day is a failure.
- **Duplicate keeper rule: a title with a parenthetical — "(set)", "(problem job)", "(5th visit)" —
  always survives.** It decided Matt Kirk, where nothing else could.
- **Problem jobs go to Cory.** Seven jobs carry "(problem job)"; capability call, not geographic, so
  they are pinned or the rebalancer hands them to whoever is nearest.
- **`balancing=ON_FORCE` was overriding the grid all along.** The optimizer reassigned stops between
  techs after the grid assigned them — the mechanism behind "why is a tech driving out of their way".
  Now planned with `--balancing=OFF`; the grid decides who, the optimizer decides order.
- 12 stale/duplicate visits deleted, each with a verified surviving visit.

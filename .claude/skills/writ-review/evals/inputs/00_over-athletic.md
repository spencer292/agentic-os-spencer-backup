The deal collapsed. Phone went dead. I sat there. Heart pounding. Three years gone. Just like that. No warning. No mercy.

I called Mike. He didn't answer. I called again. Nothing. The office was quiet. Too quiet. I packed my bag. I drove home. I told no one. I couldn't. The shame was heavy. The night was long. I didn't sleep. I stared up. The ceiling stared back.

Morning came. I got up. I tried again. I failed again. But I learned. Slowly. Painfully. I rebuilt. Brick by brick. Day by day. Client by client. And here I stand. Stronger now. Wiser now. Ready now. The lesson was simple. Never quit. Never stop. Keep going. Always.

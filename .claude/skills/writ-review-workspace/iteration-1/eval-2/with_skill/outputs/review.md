## 5. The prescription

### What's working (keep it — don't touch)
- **The focus story (D2/D6).** Diamond → "the entrepreneurs I sit with are not so different" → resolves into the personal story by para 2. It lands the point and never overstays. This is the spine; protect it.
- **Athletic dosing (D7).** "Buried, overlooked, under pressure" and "It was the pressure that made the stone" are placed exactly at peaks against a flowing baseline. This is the method's hardest balance and the piece nails it — do not add more short lines.
- **Consequence-over-journalism (P1).** "Spring of 2021" buried mid-paragraph instead of opening the book; the collapse told as "my body finally sent me the invoice." Keep that instinct.
- **The close is a call-to-mirror, not a CTA (P2/D8).** "I am not writing to pull you out. I am writing to hold up a mirror." Hold this shape.

### What's not (the soft spots, in priority order)
1. **P4 — the two anchor images are borrowed (the gate-blocker).** The diamond and the literal "hold up a mirror" are Dennis Ross's *own* examples (§6A, §1.11). The piece executes them faithfully — which is exactly why it reads a touch *template-faithful* rather than *only-this-author*. This is the single point keeping it under the 9 bar.
2. **D1 — fire is buried.** The opening prioritises warmth over tension; the real fire (the collapse) doesn't surface until para 2.
3. **D3 — statement questions under-dosed (zero).** The pivot in para 3 is a flat declaration where one beat of manufactured anticipation belongs.
4. **D8 — the mirror is named, not enacted.** "I am writing to hold up a mirror" labels the move.

### How to make it work (located, specific)
- **Fix P4 first — swap one borrowed image for one only this author owns.** Keep the diamond *or* replace it with a lived object from this author's actual collapse (a real thing in the room the day the stairs beat him). The methodology's diamond is a *seed*, not a destination; right now the piece wears the seed. One genuinely owned image moves P4 from 8→9 and clears the gate. *(Per §5: the lived detail is the human's to supply — don't let the canonical example stand in for it.)*
- **Para 3, the pivot — convert one declaration into a single statement question (fixes D3 *and* D1's buried tension at once).** "the breakdown was not the end of the story" is currently inert. Make it a no-question-mark line that forces the reader to ask *then what was it?* — one beat of anticipation immediately before "It was the pressure that made the stone." One device, at a peak, no "?". (Do **not** scatter statement questions through the piece — exactly one, here.)
- **Close — enact the mirror instead of naming it.** Cut "I am writing to hold up a mirror" down to its function: reflect the reader's life back without labelling the move, so the device works under the reader's defences rather than announcing itself. Reserve the explicit word only if it earns a deliberate beat.

---

## 6. Verdict + gate

**Overall:** A genuinely strong, *working* opening — it clears the **spirit** of the method (consequence over journalism, a real mirror, costly honesty, athletic writing correctly rationed) and avoids every common overdose trap. It is not template-correct-but-empty; it has a real collapse and a real reader-turn.

**Gate (book chapter — 9/10, no posture below 9):** **REVISE (narrow).** Postures land P1=9, P2=9, P3=9, **P4=8** — one notch under the 9 bar — and the two soft devices are D1 (fire, 7) and D3 (statement questions, 6, under-dosed). Effective score ≈ **8.5/10**.

> **For context:** at the *long-form article* gate ("no posture below 8, no device below 7") this would **PASS** but for D3=6. The piece is a strong book opening sitting just below the strict chapter bar — not a structural rebuild, a one-lever lift.

**The three fixes that move the needle, in order:**
1. **Swap one borrowed anchor image (diamond *or* mirror) for one this author alone owns** → lifts P4 8→9 and clears the gate. *(Biggest mover.)*
2. **Convert the para-3 pivot into a single statement question** → fixes the under-dosed D3 and surfaces the buried tension (D1) in one edit.
3. **Enact the close's mirror instead of naming it** → D8 8→9.

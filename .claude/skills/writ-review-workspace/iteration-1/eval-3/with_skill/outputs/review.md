## 5. The prescription

### What's working (keep it)
Honestly thin, but real:
- **It's clean, short, and grammatical** — three tight paragraphs, no purple over-writing to strip out. You're starting from a blank slate, not a mess.
- **The CTA is structurally in the right place** (the close) — keep its *position*, change its *nature* (see below).
- **One seed of a real reader consequence exists:** "free up their teams to focus on higher-value work" gestures at time/headspace. That buried idea is the raw material for the mirror — promote it.

### What's not
- **All four postures are dead** (P1–P4, every one below the gate). No consequence, no mirror, no honesty, no spirit.
- **Zero devices.** The opener is the deadest line; the close is a bare CTA.
- **There is no reader in the copy** — it's "we/our" throughout; the reflection the buyer is supposed to see is missing.
- **No differentiation.** Stock lexicon makes the company a price-taker with no unique sound.
- **No arc, no contrast** — one flat register, freely reorderable paragraphs.

### How to make it work (located, specific, Thinking-first)

1. **Before touching a line — decide the consequence and the one reader truth (§0, §1.9).** Pick the owner you're actually for, e.g. *the founder buried in repetitive work who hasn't taken a clean day off in two years*. That truth becomes the mirror and gives spirit (P4) a home. Nothing below works without this step.
2. **Replace Para 1, sentence 1** (*"At our company, we believe that AI represents a significant opportunity…"*) with an **open-with-fire** line about the *reader's* consequence, not your belief about AI. Example direction: *"You didn't start this business to spend Sundays doing data entry."* Terse, reader-facing, emotionally accurate — not a claim about the category.
3. **Turn "we/our" into "you."** Para 2's *"We have worked with many clients across a wide range of sectors"* is credentials-as-journalism — demote it from the lead and make it **one specific named consequence for one client** (a real number, a real before/after), placed lower as proof, not as the opener.
4. **Coin exactly ONE pairing for what you do (§2.3) — not five.** Give the offer/mechanism a name that leads back only to you, so you stop competing on the accountant's price sheet. Located at the key claim about your method.
5. **Pick ONE focus device up top — a focus story *or* a 90/10 reframe, not both (dosage = 1).** One 5–6 sentence analogy that puts the owner's mind in focus on the cost of staying as they are, then doubles back to them. Do not also stack statement questions into a spiderweb — this short a piece holds one.
6. **Convert the close (Para 3) from a bare CTA to a call-to-mirror that *then* earns the CTA (§3.2, §8).** Keep the free consultation — but precede it with one honest, reader-reflecting line so the ask is earned, not bolted on.
7. **Strip the toxins (§2.6) and plant one un-inventable specific (P4).** Cut "we believe **that**" and "in a way **that**"; land one real, lived detail (a client's actual saved hours, a real moment) — because in copy the spirit test is the hardest bar and right now the piece fails it outright.

## 6. Verdict + gate

**Format gate (copywriting, §8):** *no posture below 8; P4 (spirit) is the binding constraint.*

**Result: REVISE — hard fail.** Postures land at 2/2/1/1 and every device at 1–2; the spirit test fails outright. This is not a tuning job — it's a **Thinking-first rewrite**, effectively a rebuild on the same brief. The current draft's only contribution to the next version is its brevity and the buried "higher-value work" idea.

**Top 3 fixes that move the needle most:**
1. **Decide the consequence + the one reader truth, and open with it** — this single move kills the journalism, builds the mirror, and gives spirit a place to live (fixes P1, P2, P4 and D1 at once).
2. **Rewrite "we/our" into "you"** — put the reader into the copy so they close it knowing *their* situation, not your company.
3. **Convert the bare-CTA close into a call-to-mirror that earns the consultation** — keep the ask, earn it first.

**Recommended follow-on:** once rewritten, run the draft through `tool-humanizer` (deep mode if a voice profile is added) — the current spirit-dead texture is exactly what it's built to catch.

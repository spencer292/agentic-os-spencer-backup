## Standards carried from the pack currency baseline

Checked September 2026, sourced from the web-design-skill-pack shared rules.

- WCAG 2.2 level AA is the UK public sector baseline. The European Accessibility Act has applied
  to EU-facing services since 28 June 2025.
- Core Web Vitals thresholds at the 75th percentile over 28 days of field data: LCP 2.5 seconds,
  INP 200 milliseconds, CLS 0.1. INP replaced FID in March 2024.
- Baymard form findings: single-column layout, one name field rather than two, and every field
  beyond five costs 20 to 30 percent of completions.

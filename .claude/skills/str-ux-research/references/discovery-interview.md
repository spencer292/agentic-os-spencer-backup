# Client and stakeholder discovery interview

Self-contained script for `str-ux-research` Mode A. Twenty questions across seven areas. Ask at
most six per turn. Skip anything `brand_context/` already answers and say which you skipped.

No question in this script asks about timelines or effort. Sequence the work later by dependency
order and risk.

## How to run it

- Interview the decision maker first, then anyone who will veto the result. A stakeholder who is
  not interviewed becomes a late objection.
- Record disagreements rather than resolving them. Two stakeholders wanting different home pages
  is the most important thing you will learn all week.
- Capture verbatim phrasing. The language bank in the brief comes from this transcript and from
  the user interviews, nowhere else.
- Push once on every vague answer. "More leads" becomes "how many, of what kind, from where,
  worth what". If the second answer is still vague, record it as vague. That is a finding.

## Area 1: Business model and money

1. Walk me through how you make money. What does someone buy, at what price, and what happens
   after they buy?
2. Which of your services or products do you most want more of, and which do you quietly wish
   you sold less of?
3. What is a lead worth to you, and what proportion of enquiries turn into customers today?

## Area 2: Goals and success measures

4. If this site works, what is different in the business six months after it launches? Name the
   thing you would point at.
5. What number are you measuring today that would move? Where does that number live now?
6. What would make you call this project a failure, even if the site looked good?

## Area 3: Audiences

7. Who buys from you today? Describe the last three customers, not a category.
8. Who do you want to buy from you that currently does not?
9. Who else influences the decision but does not sign? Partners, procurement, a spouse, a board.
10. What do people believe about your category that is wrong, and what do you have to correct
    before they will buy?

## Area 4: Conversion definitions

11. What do you want a visitor to do on this site? Rank the actions if there is more than one.
12. What is the second-best action for someone who is interested but not ready to do the first
    thing?
13. What happens after someone converts? Who contacts them, how fast, and using what.
14. Which of these actions is currently tracked, and where does the data land?

## Area 5: Current-site pain

15. What does the current site get wrong? Be specific about pages if you can.
16. What does it get right that we must not lose? Pages, rankings, assets, phrases customers
    quote back to you.
17. What questions do prospects keep asking that the site should already have answered?

## Area 6: Brand and content

18. What do you have already? Photography, video, case studies, reviews, data, a brand guide,
    a written positioning. Which of it is current and cleared for use?
19. Whose website do you lose deals to, and what do they do better? Name three.

## Area 7: Constraints and technical reality

20. What are the hard constraints? Platform you must stay on, integrations that must keep
    working, legal or regulatory language, approvals, accessibility obligations, domains and
    URLs that cannot change, anyone who must sign off.

## Follow-ups worth having ready

Use these when an answer opens a door.

- On goals: "How would you know that had happened without asking me?"
- On audiences: "What did that customer try before they found you?"
- On conversion: "What stops someone calling instead of filling in the form?"
- On current pain: "Show me the page you dislike most and tell me why."
- On constraints: "Who has stopped a project like this before, and over what?"

## Output of Mode A

Write these into the brief and nothing else from the interview:

- Business goals and the measures behind them
- Ranked conversion actions with their tracking status
- Audience list, marked as client-stated until user interviews confirm them
- Stakeholder conflicts, both positions stated
- Assets available and cleared
- Constraints, each marked hard or soft
- Language bank, verbatim
- Open questions, each with the person who can answer it

Everything in the audience list that came only from this interview is a hypothesis. Label it.

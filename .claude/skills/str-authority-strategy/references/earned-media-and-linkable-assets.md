# Earned Media + Linkable Assets

> **CLIENT CONTEXT:** this is a Got Moles client skill. Every outlet, tier and asset example below is Western Washington and US-market. Got Moles is a mole-control service business with three Google Business Profiles (Seattle, Tacoma, Enumclaw), 219+ five-star Google reviews, nearly 5,000 properties served across 6 counties and 92+ communities, founded 2017 by Spencer Hill (US Army veteran; his 15+ years of personal mole-control experience is distinct from the company's founding date). Never write "WA's #1" and never claim I-713 compliance. US English throughout.

Steps 6 and 7 of `str-authority-strategy`. Earned media is where the brand gets mentioned; linkable assets are the reason anyone mentions it.

## Why these two pair together

- **Linkable asset** = the thing people want to cite
- **Earned media** = where they cite it

An asset without distribution is invisible. Outreach without an asset is begging, and editors ignore it. The pair is the engine.

## The priority class: third-party ranked listicles

**[S] Ranked listicles are ~21% of all AI citations — the single most-cited format** (arXiv 2606.20065, 100,000+ prompt responses across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini). **[S] Whitespark's 2026 local ranking factors name expert-curated "best of" lists as a top driver of the new AI-visibility dimension.**

For a local service business this outranks conventional link building. The target set is concrete: "best mole removal in {city}", "best pest control in {county}", "top-rated lawn care in {metro}" across the priority cities in `target-keywords.md`.

Per roundup capture:
- **Publisher** and URL
- **Query it ranks for** and which priority city it serves
- **Inclusion path** — editorial pick, submission form, directory-derived, or pay-to-play
- **Current occupants** — which competitors are listed
- **Status** — target / submitted / included / declined

**Rules.** Pursue editorial and open-submission roundups. Directory-derived roundups (many are generated from Yelp, BBB or Angi data) are won by fixing the underlying directory profile, which is Section 10 work. **Never pay for a slot in a roundup presented as editorial, and never fabricate an entry.** A paid placement disclosed as advertising is a media buy, not earned authority — label it as such if it is ever proposed.

## Earned-media target list (Step 6)

For each cluster from `target-keywords.md`, list outlets passing three filters:

1. **Topic coverage** — they regularly cover the cluster's subject
2. **Audience overlap** — Western Washington homeowners and property managers, or the trade that refers them
3. **Authority that matters** — regional recognition, or an AI citation footprint

### Tiering by access difficulty

| Tier | Outlets for this client | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — flagship regional and national** | Seattle Times, Tacoma News Tribune, The Olympian, Pacific NW Magazine, Seattle Magazine; nationally Better Homes & Gardens or Real Simple as a quoted expert | Original regional data plus a Tier 2/3 stack as proof |
| **Tier 2 — industry and trade** | Pest Control Technology, Pest Management Professional, Lawn & Landscape, veteran-business publications, regional business journals | Achievable with original data, an expert quote or a contributed byline |
| **Tier 3 — niche, local and community** | County Chamber newsletters (King, Pierce, Snohomish, Thurston, Kitsap, Lewis), Enumclaw Courier-Herald and other city papers, PNW landscaping podcasts and YouTube channels, WSU Master Gardener publications, local subreddits, Nextdoor | Most achievable; accumulated volume is what compounds |

Most strategies fail by chasing Tier 1 first. Build the Tier 3 stack, let Tier 2 follow, and approach Tier 1 with proof points in hand.

### YouTube is an earned surface, not a repurposing channel

**[S] YouTube mentions are the strongest single correlate with AI visibility across ChatGPT (0.737), AI Mode (0.712) and AI Overviews (0.740)** — ahead of branded web mentions. In the arXiv citation study, YouTube is the leading non-corporate source, ahead of Reddit, editorial media and Wikipedia.

Treat it as a Tier 2/3 earned-media surface in its own right:
- Guest appearances and collaborations with PNW lawn-care, gardening and homesteading channels
- Both channels named in title, description and transcript — that is where the mention is read
- The footage doubles as proof for the **[S] 97% of consumers who verify an AI recommendation against real evidence before acting**

### Per-outlet capture

- **Outlet name**
- **Tier** (1/2/3)
- **Cluster and topic match** — which of the seven cluster ids, and why
- **Audience overlap** — full or partial, and which ICP segment
- **Pitch angle** — the specific story this outlet would publish, never generic
- **Submission path** — editorial guidelines URL, reporter beat, podcast or channel booking page
- **Status** — target / contacted / pitched / placed

### Anti-patterns

- **Generic outlet lists.** No per-outlet angle means the row never gets used.
- **National-first bias.** The ICP is Western Washington homeowners. A regional paper beats a national title for this brand.
- **Chasing dead outlets.** Verify that guest contributions are still accepted before pitching.
- **Counting reach without authority.** A 20K-reader outlet that engines cite beats a 100K-reader outlet they never retrieve.
- **Any tactic that hides who paid.** See the prohibition in `co-citation-and-brand-mentions.md`.

## Linkable asset patterns (Step 7)

Linkable assets are built to be cited. The hook matters more than the format.

### Working asset types for this client

| Type | Why it earns citations | Got Moles example |
|---|---|---|
| **Original dataset from operations** | A number nobody else has, at regional scale | Western Washington mole-damage data drawn from the job history across nearly 5,000 properties: peak activity months by county, damage patterns, before and after |
| **Definitive regional guide** | Becomes the canonical reference for a jurisdiction-specific question | Washington legal mole-trapping guide, citing WDFW and the relevant RCW and WAC provisions |
| **Field guide / identification asset** | Educators, gardeners and extension programs link to it | The three Washington mole species — Townsend's, Pacific and shrew mole — with photography, habitat and identification |
| **Calculator or interactive tool** | Functional utility gets shared and cited | Mole-damage cost estimator scoped to Western Washington cities |
| **Tested-methods study** | Counter-narrative with a stated method | DIY mole methods tested and ranked, with a documented protocol and a video companion |
| **Seasonal reference** | Recurring annual citation surface | Pacific Northwest mole activity calendar, updated each season |
| **Case study set** | B2B referral magnet | One case study per commercial vertical: HOA, sports field, school grounds |
| **Founder profile** | Press hook and entity evidence at once | Spencer's US Army-to-business story, with photography and timeline |

### Hook engineering

Every asset needs the angle that makes it more citable than a generic guide. Five shapes:

1. **Original number** — "X% of Western Washington yards we treated showed damage in month Y", from real job data
2. **Counter-narrative** — the DIY method everyone recommends, tested and failing
3. **First-of-kind** — the first regional measurement or the first jurisdiction-specific guide
4. **Insider data** — proprietary access to the operation's own history
5. **Synthesis** — pulling extension-service, regulatory and field data into one picture

A guide without a hook is content. A guide with a hook is a linkable asset.

### Sizing and sequencing — no effort or duration estimates

**Do not attach hours, days, weeks, "quick win" or effort bands to any asset.** Rank and sequence by:

- **Impact** — which surface it earns on (roundup inclusion, press pickup, AI Overviews, AI Mode, classic organic) and how many clusters it serves
- **Dependency** — what must exist first: data pulled and cleaned, photography shot, video filmed, legal review, a prior asset used as a proof point
- **Risk and reversibility** — an asset making a factual claim about pricing, legality or outcomes carries a correction risk; one built from published sources does not
- **Reuse** — whether it feeds a YouTube companion, city-page proof blocks and the hallucination-correction surfaces in Section 8.4

Sequence by what unblocks what. The founder profile and the species field guide unblock press conversations; press placements unblock the entity-notability work in Section 9; the operations dataset is the tentpole those conversations are built toward.

### Per-asset capture

- **Asset type and working title**
- **Cluster served** — must match a `target-keywords.md` cluster id
- **Hook** — one sentence
- **Citation potential** — which surface, and why an engine or editor would quote it
- **Dependency** — what must exist first
- **YouTube companion** — required for any visual topic
- **Distribution plan** — which roundups and which Tier 1/2/3 outlets get it
- **Status** — planned / in production / published / amplifying

## How earned media and linkable assets execute together

Per cluster:
1. Pick the cluster's primary asset and name its hook.
2. Ship the assets with no upstream dependency first, and use them as proof points in outlet conversations.
3. Use each placement as the notability evidence that Section 9 needs.
4. Pitch the tentpole asset (the operations dataset, the tested-methods study) once the proof stack exists.
5. Pair every visual asset with a YouTube upload — that is the highest-correlating signal available.
6. Log every placement, roundup inclusion and mention in Section 8's tracking so velocity and sentiment are computable.

Every asset is the spine of an ongoing earned-media push for its cluster. Without the asset, the outlet list is a wish list.

## Note on repurposing

No content-repurposing skill is installed in this workspace. Atomizing an asset into social posts is done directly, or through `mkt-authority-content` for long-form derivatives. Repurposing amplifies an asset; it never substitutes for building one.

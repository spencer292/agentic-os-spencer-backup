# Toward the Thing You're Avoiding — draft manuscript (chapters 1–3)

_Working spine: the size of your life is set by the size of the thing you're willing to walk toward instead of away from._

## Chapter 1 — Seventy-Five Pounds

I had seventy-five pounds and an interview for a job I had no business getting. Spreadsheet operator, and I did not know what a spreadsheet was. The honest move was to walk away and find something I could already do. I did the other thing.

I spent seventy of the seventy-five on a twenty-minute crash course in the shop I happened to pass, walked into the BBC with five pounds and a head full of borrowed knowledge, and got the job. The risk was small in pounds and enormous in nerve.

That is where it started. Walk toward the thing, and the door tends to open.

## Chapter 2 — The Bill for Ninety-Hour Weeks

Twenty years later the avoidance had changed shape. I was not avoiding risk anymore. I was avoiding *stillness* — because stillness is where you have to feel how you've been living. So I filled every hour. Ninety-hour weeks. In 2021 my body sent the invoice and I nearly ended up in hospital.

Recovery did not come from working less. It came from doing the thing I had spent a decade outrunning: sitting with myself, in the cold, in the quiet, and asking why. Breathwork. Ice. A hard conversation with the man in the mirror.

The stakes were higher this time. Not five pounds — my health, my family, the whole shape of my life. Same move, though. Walk toward the thing.

## Chapter 3 — Under the Ice

The deepest version of this I found under a frozen lake in Finland. One breath, no tank, a hole in the ice above you and a rope to follow back. Every instinct screams to turn around. The whole discipline is staying calm while everything in you wants to flee.

Down there I understood what the seventy-five pounds and the burnout had both been teaching, in smaller print. The largest life is not the one with the least fear. It is the one where you have made friends with walking toward fear instead of away from it.

The door tends to open. It opened in a shop doorway in London, it opened in a cold bath in 2021, and it opened under the ice. Same door. It has been the same door the whole time.

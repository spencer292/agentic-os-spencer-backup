## Chapter 1 — My Morning Routine

I wake at five. Cold shower, then twenty minutes of breathwork before anyone else is up. I drink a litre of water before coffee. I write three things I am grateful for. None of this is original and all of it works. A morning you control is a day you control. I have kept this routine for three years and it has changed how I show up to everything.

By six I have done more for myself than I used to do all week. People think discipline is hard. It is only hard at the start. After that it is just who you are.

## Chapter 2 — The Time I Jumped Out of a Plane in Spain

I have done over two thousand five hundred skydives. This particular one was in Spain, a hot afternoon, a malfunction at four thousand feet. The main canopy spun up and I had seconds to decide. I cut away and pulled the reserve. It opened. I landed in a field next to a very surprised farmer.

Skydiving teaches you to make decisions under pressure. You do not get to negotiate with gravity. There is something clarifying about that. When the worst case is right in front of you, the noise in your head goes quiet.

## Chapter 3 — How to Build a Spreadsheet That Doesn't Break

Most spreadsheets fall apart because people mix data and calculations on the same sheet. Keep your raw data on one tab and your working on another. Name your ranges. Never hard-code a number inside a formula. Lock the cells you don't want touched.

I built my first business on spreadsheets nobody else could read, which was a mistake. A model only has value if someone else can pick it up and trust it.

## Chapter 4 — Burnout

In 2021 I nearly put myself in hospital. Ninety-hour weeks, no sleep, running on adrenaline and ego. The body keeps the score and mine was sending the invoice. I recovered through breathwork and cold exposure and a hard look at why I was working that way in the first place.

Burnout is not a badge. It is a bill. I paid it and I would rather you didn't have to.

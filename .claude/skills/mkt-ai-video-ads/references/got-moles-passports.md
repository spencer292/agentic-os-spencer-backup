# Got Moles Passports and Look Block

The course's consistency method: write a "passport" for every recurring person, object and place.
Paste the same text into every prompt, attach the same reference image, and the brand stays
consistent across shots and across ads. Keep the passports here. Update them when Spencer
supplies real photos, which always beat descriptions.

## Look block (paste into every photoreal prompt)

> Western Washington suburb in the Puget Sound area, soft overcast Pacific Northwest daylight,
> diffused light with no harsh shadows, damp deep-green lawn, Douglas firs and cedars in the
> background, wet pavement sheen, natural muted color grade, realistic smartphone or documentary
> camera, slight handheld movement, real textures, no cinematic teal-orange grade, no studio
> lighting.

Seasonal variants: **fall** (Oct–Nov) wet leaves, low gray sky, early dusk. **Spring** (Mar–May)
fresh bright-green growth, brief sun breaks. **Winter** (Dec–Feb) frost on the grass, bare maples.
Mole hills show up most in fall and spring rain.

# Editorial Pass — *Thinking Outside Your Brain*
_Reviewed: the whole file (`book-thinking-outside-your-brain.md`, 1,077 lines). Methodology: Dennis Ross "Lead With Words" (v2). Voice baseline: `voice-profile-book.md`. Reader: `icp.md` (Mark/Mary)._

> **Scope note — read this first.** The file is a Notion *knowledge-base* export, not a clean book file. It bundles three different things: (1) **the book proper** — Foreword + Chapters 1–10 across Parts One–Three; (2) **two teaching modules** — "The Journey of AI Mastery (Four Stages of Competence)" and "AI Across Every Business Function" (7 role sections); and (3) **a standalone concept essay** — "The Owner-Architect Principle," which carries its own "Reusability notes" saying it's designed to flex across book / training / LinkedIn / podcast / keynote. This managing-editor pass scores **the book proper (Foreword + Ch1–10)** as the manuscript, and treats the trailing modules as the back-matter they appear to be. That distinction is itself the report's biggest finding: most of the repetition, pacing-tail and voice-drift deductions below trace to those appended modules overlapping the finished book. The single highest-leverage decision is to formally separate book from back-matter.

**M7 Voice consistency — 7/10.** Where the book is drafted (Laura, Mary, balance board, dinner, the Ch1–10 quoted passages) it is unmistakably one author — flowing baseline, consequence-led, athletic landings held for the peak. Two deductions: (1) the manuscript is **unevenly drafted** — large stretches (much of the Foreword, the stats sections, the function sections) are knowledge-base notes/bullets, not prose, so the connective tissue has no voice yet; (2) where the back-matter *is* drafted, it reads in a more commercial register — the Functions lesson as a workbook/deck, the Owner-Architect essay as a punchy op-ed — a notch off the reflective memoir-method baseline of the core chapters.

## The Structural Prescription

**What's working (protect):**
- The Laura → dinner spine and the Cage → open-door payoff. Don't touch the open or the close.
- EVOLVE as a clean six-chapter backbone — each letter a distinct job, building in order. This is the book's strongest structural asset.
- The deliberate callbacks (George, the brain dump, "Decide. Connect. Create.", "release not replace"). They give the book cohesion — keep every one.
- The finished narrative set-pieces (Laura, Mary, balance board, dinner). This is Roy's voice at full strength — they are the bar the rest should be drafted up to.

**What's not:**
- The file bundles a finished book (Foreword + Ch1–10) with teaching modules and a standalone essay that overlap the book and are written in a more commercial register.
- Ch8 and the Owner-Architect essay develop the same architect thesis twice; Ch8's org-chart and the Functions lesson are the same material long and short; Ch10 and the Functions module both close on the brain-dump CTA.
- The book is unevenly drafted — peaks are prose, much connective tissue is still knowledge-base notes/bullets.
- A few continuity wobbles (adoption stats, the verb list, the Mary name).
- A teaching plateau across Ch4–8 and an expository tail after the emotional climax.

**The moves (highest leverage first, each located):**
1. **Decide book vs back-matter, and cut the duplicate architect development.** Designate Foreword + Ch1–10 as the book. Move "The Journey of AI Mastery (Four Stages)" and "AI Across Every Business Function" to a clearly-labelled appendix/companion (or out of the book). Fold *only* the unique "find the who / signals of a good who / start small" material from the Owner-Architect essay into the back of **Ch8**, and cut the essay's architect setup that Ch8 already does. **This one decision lifts M4, M5 and M7 together** — it is the highest-leverage edit in the manuscript.
2. **Get expository material out of the post-climax position.** Nothing flat should sit after the dinner-table close (Ch10). If the Functions catalogue stays in the book, it goes *before* Ch9–10 or into an appendix — never after the emotional landing.
3. **Lift the V-O-L-V-E middle (Ch4–8) off its plateau.** Surface one genuine emotional peak in the Ch6–8 stretch: the facilities-management quote disaster (Ch6) and Spencer's story (Ch8) both carry latent emotional charge currently played only for instruction — let one land as a peak so the middle isn't six chapters at one altitude.
4. **Close the continuity items.** Add a scoping clause so Ch1's 88% and Ch9's <1-in-6 can't read as contradictory; fix the "Decide. Connect. Create.(/Feel.)" list to one canonical form; rename the Ch3 employee *or* the Mark/Mary reader avatar; confirm 25 vs 28 years and the $ in Spencer's story.
5. **Finish the prose.** Convert the note-form / bullet connective tissue (much of the Foreword, the stats sections, the function sections) into Roy's drafted voice — or route those passages to `writ-draft`. Until they're prose, pacing and voice can't be fully judged.
6. **Pay back the parenting metaphor.** The birth → grow → teenage → release-the-business image opened in the Foreword isn't closed; a one-line callback in Ch10 (the business finally released without destroying its creator) resolves it.
7. **Per-chapter craft is `writ-review`'s job.** Where individual sections need line-level lifting (e.g., the Functions sections' workbook voice), run `writ-review` on them — that critique does not belong in this manuscript-level pass.

## Verdict

**REVISE.** The book underneath is coherent, well-arced and emotionally landed — a real book with a nameable spine and a delivered transformation. It is not failed on either blocking defect: the through-line is clear (M1 fine) and there is no irreconcilable contradiction (M3's one open item closes with a scoping clause). It does not clean-pass the gate because M4, M5 and M7 sit below 8 — and, importantly, **all three trace to the same root cause:** the file bundles the finished book with overlapping teaching modules and is unevenly drafted.

**Top 3 moves that move the needle most:**
1. **Separate book from back-matter and de-duplicate the architect thesis** (Ch8 ↔ Owner-Architect; Ch8 org chart ↔ Functions lesson). One decision; lifts M4, M5 and M7 at once.
2. **Get the expository modules out of the post-climax tail and lift the Ch4–8 plateau** with one surfaced peak.
3. **Finish the note-form sections into prose and close the five continuity items.**

**Blocking flags:** none fatal. The only continuity item that must be closed before a clean pass is the adoption-stat juxtaposition (Ch1 88% vs Ch9 <1-in-6) — fixable with a one-line scope, not a rewrite.

_Recommended follow-up: run `writ-review` on the V-O-L-V-E chapters (4–8) and on any back-matter that survives into the book, to lift per-chapter craft once the structure above is settled._

# Structured Interview Guide — {Role} — {Candidate} — {Date}

Same questions, same order, for every candidate. Score as you go, before comparing candidates.
Anchors: **1** = no evidence · **3** = adequate, would not worry · **5** = strong, specific, repeated evidence.

## Do not ask
Family, children, childcare, age, health, disability, religion, national origin, citizenship
(beyond "authorised to work in the US"), arrests, or **what they currently earn** (WA salary history
ban, all employer sizes). If it comes up unprompted, do not record it and do not weigh it.

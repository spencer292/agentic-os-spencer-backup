# Touch Menu and the 10-Week Arc

## The arc

| Week | Aim | Typical touch |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get noticed | LinkedIn comment on their post, or a short hello and a connect if we're not connected yet |
| 2 | Be useful | Email or DM with something they can use: mole pressure in their area this fall, a local tip, an article |
| 3 | **First lunch ask** | "I'd like to buy you lunch. No pitch, I just like knowing the people in this industry." |
| 4 | Give | An introduction to someone who helps *them* |
| 5 | In person | Coffee drop-off at the office or clubhouse (Thanksgiving week works well) |
| 6 | Give | Mention them or their property in a LinkedIn post (ask first), or share their post with a real comment |
| 7 | **Second lunch ask** | Different framing: coffee near their office, or meet at an association event |
| 8 | Light | Comment or a short check-in |
| 9 | Mail | Handwritten holiday card |
| 10 | Close | "Door's open anytime. Happy new year." Fall-off if there's no meeting |

Adjust when they reply. A reply always gets an answer the same week, and the arc follows the conversation. A warm whale already in conversation can skip straight to the lunch ask.

## Channel rules

- Don't use the same channel two weeks running.
- At most one ask per message.
- Keep DMs under about 300 characters; email body under about 120 words.
- Give before you ask. Week 3 is the earliest lunch ask for a cold whale.

## Spencer's voice: examples

Good (his register):
> Hey Liam! Hope the fall's treating you OK. The rain brought the mounds back right on schedule over in Lacey. I'd love to buy you lunch sometime, no business, just good to know the folks in this world. Give me a shout!

Bad (agency register, don't write like this):
> The useful version is this: fall mole pressure lands on your managers' desks right when budgets close. Happy to be a resource either way.

## Gives that cost nothing and commit no one

- Introductions: a striper (Nick Granberg), an irrigation contact, another property manager, a vendor they're missing.
- Local intelligence: "We're seeing heavy activity in Sammamish this week."
- Recognition: a genuine comment, congratulations on a promotion or certification, sharing their post.
- An invite: to an association event Spencer is already attending.

Not gives (each needs a separate decision from Spencer): free inspections, free visits, discounts, staff time.

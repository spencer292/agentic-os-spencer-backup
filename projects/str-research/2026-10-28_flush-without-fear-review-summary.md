# Flush Without Fear Plumbing and Drain LLC: Google Review Summary

Pulled 2026-10-28 from the Google Search reviews panel. The listing shows **5.0 stars across 207 reviews**. About 100 of those have written text, and all 100 were captured, covering roughly 5 years. The rest are star-only ratings. Raw text: `2026-10-28_flush-without-fear-google-reviews-raw.txt`.

## What customers say about Brian

Brian is named in roughly 70 of the 100 written reviews. The same themes come up again and again:

1. **He shows up when he says he will.** Punctuality is the most common praise. He texts or calls when he's on the way, and sometimes arrives early. One reviewer wrote, "right on time for the appointment (when does that happen nowadays)."
2. **He's honest and doesn't upsell.** Reviewers use the phrases "doesn't believe in upselling," "no up selling, just straight to what needed to be done," and "no surprises or extra charges." Several say he talked them into the cheaper fix, for example repairing a water heater instead of replacing it. Others mention he skipped charging for work he couldn't finish that day.
3. **His prices are fair, and often lower than competitors'.** One customer wrote that he "came in so far below the others I was like, 'Please!! Take my money.'" Free estimates and phone quotes come up often. One reviewer compared that to other plumbers who "charge $99 just to give an estimate."
4. **He explains the problem.** Customers say he walks them through what's wrong, lays out options that fit their budget, and separates a quick fix from a long-term fix. One woman who rents rooms to other women called him "very trustworthy to work with."
5. **He answers the phone and responds fast in emergencies.** Reviewers describe him answering after 7pm, on Saturday nights, on Sunday mornings, on holiday weekends and on Easter weekend, often arriving within an hour. One customer had called two other companies, got no answer, and never heard back from either.
6. **He's clean and careful.** He leaves the work area "cleaner than when he came in," cuts only the drywall holes he needs, and is good with customers' dogs.
7. **He stands behind his work.** Reviewers mention a 90-day guarantee, a 6-month warranty, and one customer got a one-year personal 24/7 warranty on a water heater.
8. **He fixes what others couldn't.** Several reviews say an earlier plumber or a warranty company's contractor botched the job and Brian fixed it. One customer had been given "the run around by five other people who took months."

Customers come back. Many reviews are from repeat customers, and the same phrases keep appearing: "my go-to plumber," "customer for life," "the kind of plumber whose number you save."

## What sets them apart from the competition

- **Owner-operator trust.** People feel they're hiring Brian personally, not a dispatch company. One reviewer wrote, "Glad to support a local independent tradesman." It's the main thing customers mention.
- **Steering customers to the cheaper fix.** Big shops are known for upselling, and customers notice when Brian doesn't.
- **After-hours availability for a small shop.** He takes nights, weekends and holiday calls. Competitors in reviews don't answer.
- **Warranties on top of the work.** Personal warranties go beyond the job itself.
- **Military/veteran discount**, mentioned unprompted by two customers.
- **He handles permits and large jobs.** That includes whole-home repipes (PolyB replacement), well systems and remodel plumbing. One customer said he did "every part of the plumbing" and handled the county permit.
- **The owner replies to every review.** Recent replies are detailed and personal.

## Weak spots

- **One clearly negative review** (Rob Sutton, about 2 months ago) of a remodel. It describes a mismeasured tub and spout, loose supply lines in the garage, leftover materials, and Brian acknowledging the problems without offering a fix. The owner replied, accepted responsibility, and offered corrective work.
- As the company has grown, more recent reviews credit Scotty and Zack instead of Brian. The personal Brian experience is becoming a crew experience, which is a growing pain worth watching.
- One customer said they "weren't the cheapest" but chose them anyway for follow-through and no upselling.

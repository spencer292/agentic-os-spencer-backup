# LinkedIn Volume Plan — 2026-10-20

## Why it isn't working

The approach isn't failing. It has barely been run.

| | Actual (08-31 to 10-14) | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | 4 in 7 weeks | 14 at twice weekly |
| Invites sent | about 20 | about 100 per week (LinkedIn cap) |
| Prospects in pipeline | 25, 16 of them golf | — |
| Result | 2 golf conversations from about 7 cold notes, 1 phone call (Oien), 1 site-visit request that lapsed (Gordon) | — |

That is about 3% of the available invite volume. Every prospect got deep research, a hand-written note and a four-touch ladder, so each one cost about 20 minutes. At that cost per person, the pipeline can't grow.

Two leaks besides volume:
1. **No ask.** Messages built rapport but didn't propose a specific next step. Gordon asked for a visit himself, and it still lapsed.
2. **Stale invites.** 13 invites are pending, some since 09-10. A large pending backlog lowers acceptance rate, and LinkedIn uses acceptance rate to set limits.

## The change: volume, with tiered personalization

Target **50 invites a week** (half the cap, leaving room for safety), sent in two sessions of about 25.

| Tier | Who | Treatment | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — portfolio | Multi-site buyers: PM firms, multi-course operators (Arcis, Oki, KemperSports, Troon), school district grounds leads, cemetery groups | Full research + note, as now | ~10% |
| B — segment | Single-site decision makers | Bare invite. After they accept: a segment message plus one local anchor (Jobber client count in their city or on their street, pulled by script) | ~90% |

Tier B messages are about 80% shared per segment and 20% specific to the person. Spencer approves each segment message once, then approves the batch list. This replaces approving each message one by one.

**Every first message ends with one ask:** a 15-minute call, or a grounds walk with a commercial quote (Cory runs the walk). No free work is offered (rule from 2026-09-10).

## Weekly rhythm (about 2 × 75 min)

**Session A (Mon/Tue):**
1. Read the inbox, invitations and recent connections.
2. Withdraw invites older than 21 days.
3. Answer replies.
4. Source 2 segments, 25 prospects.
5. Prior-contact check.
6. Approve, then send.

**Session B (Thu):**
1. Same opening.
2. Send first messages to new accepts.
3. Work the ladder.
4. Source 25 more.
5. Approve, then send.

Search budget: about 6 searches per session, about 50 per month. That is well under the free limit of about 300.

Ladder change: keep the 4-touch ceiling, but tier B gets 3 touches (days 1/5/12). Volume replaces persistence.

## LinkedIn content (added 2026-10-20)

Content supports the outreach. A prospect who gets an invite clicks Spencer's profile, and what they find there decides whether they accept. Recent field posts from a mole specialist do more than an empty profile with a good note.

- **2 posts a week**, photo-led, in Spencer's voice. Rotate the posts through the segments being worked that week, so a prospect who gets an invite sees a recent post about a property like theirs.
- **Photo source:** a Google Drive folder that Spencer is setting up (2026-10-20). Folder link: _pending_.
- **Raw material:** real jobs. Commercial grounds, HOA common areas, a field with a mound hazard, a catch photo where it's appropriate, before and after. Phone photos beat designed graphics on LinkedIn.
- **Post ideas by segment:**
  - Golf: "what a mound does to a green collar"
  - Sports fields: "ankle hazard on a youth field"
  - HOA/PM: "the board complaint that keeps coming back"
  - Cemeteries: "mounds on graves, and why chemical-free matters there"
  - Landscapers: "the callbacks your crews keep eating"
- **Engagement:** each session, comment on 5–10 prospects' posts. It's free and doesn't count against any limit.
- **Mechanics:** drafts come from `00-social-content` / `mkt-social-showing`, then go through `tool-humanizer`, then Spencer approves. Zernio has no credentials on this install, so posts go up through the logged-in browser with confirmation, or Spencer posts them himself.
- **Claims gate applies:** use "283+ five-star reviews" only after re-measuring, never "WA's #1", no I-713 claims, no body-gripping or kill wording.

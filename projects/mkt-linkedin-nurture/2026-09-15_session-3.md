## Mechanical finding — the composer lives in an iframe

Every composer selector failed against `document` while the box was plainly visible in a screenshot.
LinkedIn renders the whole messaging UI inside a same-origin `/preload/?_bprMode=vanilla` iframe.
`elementFromPoint()` over the composer returning an `IFRAME` is the tell. Full working pattern —
including `execCommand('insertText')` instead of `innerText =`, which is what keeps Send enabled —
is now in `context/learnings.md` → `## tool-browser`.

## Two near-misses worth recording

**1 · The profile page offers you Connect buttons for other people.** On Joel Kachmarek's profile,
`main button` returned eight buttons reading exactly "Connect" — and **not one of them was Joel**.
They were all sidebar suggestions. The first would have invited a James Hochrine. The subject's own
Connect is an `<a>`, not a `<button>`, so a button scan misses it entirely. The fix is to key on
`aria-label`, which spells out "Invite <Full Name> to connect".

**2 · The name guard fired and saved an invite.** The send script verifies the intended name appears
in the modal before typing. On Renee Geyer it aborted — her aria-label carries a ", CGCS" suffix that
broke the name regex. Nothing was sent, the matcher was widened, and it went out correctly on retry.

Both findings, plus the shadow-root mechanics of the whole Connect flow, are in
`context/learnings.md` → `## tool-browser`.

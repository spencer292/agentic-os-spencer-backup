# Coffee Roasting — Side Business Exploration

## Goal

Find out, for under $1,800 and a capped number of hours, whether small-batch coffee
roasting is a real business for Spencer on the SR-410 / Plateau corridor — or a
good hobby that should stay one. Decide on evidence (repeat purchases), not
enthusiasm.

## Deliverables

- `2026-09-15_roasting-business-plan.md` — full rundown: start, unit economics,
  market analysis, scaling ladder, success factors, the coffee-shop question,
  gates and kill criteria.
- `unit-economics.mjs` — live margin model. Edit the inputs at the top, run
  `node unit-economics.mjs` to re-price a bag, a month, and the Bullet payback.
- `roast-log.csv` — (to create at Stage 0) one row per roast. This file is the
  single biggest predictor of whether this works.

## Acceptance criteria

The exploration has succeeded when ONE of these is true, whichever comes first:

- **Go:** by month 6, 20+ people have bought twice at full price.
- **Stop:** by month 6, fewer than 10 people have bought twice at full price —
  or any month where coffee takes more than 8 hours Got Moles needed.

Either outcome is a win. An unresolved "maybe" at month 12 is the only failure.

## Constraints

- **Attention, not cash, is the binding constraint.** Got Moles has a vacant
  Marketing seat, a vacant Finance seat, and an open phone-conversion gap.
  Hard-cap the weekly hours and write the cap down.
- **Behmor ceiling is real:** ~1.5-2 lb roasted/hr including cooling cycles.
  Caps the whole venture near $1,500-2,000/mo revenue. It is a school, not a
  business machine.
- **Cottage Food permit is direct-to-consumer, in-state only** — no wholesale,
  no third-party retail, no out-of-state shipping, ~$35k annual gross cap
  (VERIFY current figure with WSDA).
- **Shipping destroys DTC margin** at this bag price. Local pickup, farmers
  markets and local delivery only, until volume changes the math.
- **Separate LLC.** Do not run this through Rainier Power Wash LLC.
- **Brand firewall:** no Got Moles branding on coffee, no mass-texting the mole
  customer list. That list is a gift-and-invite channel; one clumsy blast spends
  trust earned over nine years.

## Dependencies / open items

- [ ] Confirm with WSDA: cottage food cap, current fee, roasted coffee on the
      allowed list, exact home-kitchen label disclosure wording.
- [ ] Confirm with Puget Sound Clean Air Agency: roaster size threshold for a
      Notice of Construction and afterburner. Needed BEFORE any Stage 3 lease,
      not after. Non-issue at Behmor scale (built-in catalytic converter).
- [ ] Check city zoning / home occupation permit (Buckley).
- [ ] Ask accountant how tech-delivered gift bags would be expensed, and whether

- [ ] Green coffee: open accounts at Sweet Maria's or Happy Mug now;
      Genuine Origin (single 66 lb boxes, no minimum, published cupping scores)
      is the Stage 2 bridge that roughly halves green cost.

## Stage gates

| Gate | Trigger | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| 0 -> 1 | 40+ logged roasts, one coffee reproduced 3x running | Start selling |
| 1 -> 2 | 20 repeat full-price buyers | Cottage food permit, farmers market |
| 2 -> 3 | Sold out 4 CONSECUTIVE weeks | Buy the Aillio Bullet (~$3,800) |
| 3 -> 4 | Wholesale profitable on full-bag green AND someone else can run a shift | Consider commercial space / drive-thru |

Buy the Bullet on four consecutive sold-out weeks. Not on excitement, not on a deal.

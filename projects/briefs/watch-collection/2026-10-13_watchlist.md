# Watch Collection — Watchlist & Deal Rules

Personal project. Owned: **Citizen Eco-Drive Garrison BM8180-03E** (field watch, solar, NATO). That fills the field-watch slot, so the list builds out the rest of a mixed collection: diver, dress, digital beater, chronograph, GMT, and later a luxury anchor.

Selection test for every watch: (1) you'd wear it for years, (2) it beats its price on finish, movement or heritage, (3) it holds value on the used market, so it can be traded later.

Prices are USD estimates from my knowledge as of mid-2026 (ESTIMATE). The hunter re-prices every find against live eBay sold comps, and those comps win over the numbers here.

## Deal rules the hunter uses

- **Deal:** used ≥15% below the median of the last ~10 eBay sold comps for the same reference and condition, or new ≥20% below MSRP from an authorized or reputable gray dealer.
- **Hot deal:** ≥25% below comps/MSRP. Listed first in the digest.
- **Fees count:** price includes shipping and estimated tax.
- **Fake gate (Tier 4–5, and any Tudor/Omega/Rolex/Grand Seiko):** listings from Craigslist, OfferUp, Mercari, Poshmark or Facebook are only alerted with seller history, real photos (caseback, serial area, movement if possible) and box/papers, always tagged **AUTHENTICATE BEFORE PAYING**. A price more than 40% below market on a luxury watch is treated as a likely fake or scam, not a deal.
- **Seller gate:** eBay ≥98% feedback with 50+ sales; r/Watchexchange seller has timestamp photo + trade-flair history; marketplace sellers local to Seattle–Tacoma for in-person meetups only (meet at a jeweler or a bank).
- **Skip:** Frankenwatches, aftermarket dials/bezels (unless listed as a mod at a mod price), "for parts", no-photo listings, wire-transfer-only.

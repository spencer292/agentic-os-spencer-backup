## The organization
- Associa EMB is the Puget Sound branch of Associa (Dallas), the largest HOA manager in the US. Redmond-based; serves Metro Seattle, Eastside and Puget Sound.
- Parent figures vary by page: 225 to 300+ branches, 11,000 to 15,000+ team members, 5 to 7.5 million residents. EMB's own count of communities or homes: not found (unverified). Ask at lunch.
- How they buy: individual association boards approve vendors; the management company recommends and often keeps a preferred-vendor list. Corporate vendor-onboarding rules (insurance, W-9): details unverified. Boards finalize 2027 budgets this fall.
- Practical read: a bid is usually won by a manager recommending you to a board, so the managers who trust you matter as much as branch leaders.

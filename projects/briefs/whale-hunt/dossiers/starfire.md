# Starfire Sports — Whale Dossier

**Primary:** Eric Olmstead, President & CEO · **Other contacts:** Antonio Alfaro, Director of Facilities (directory, unverified); Chris Slatt, Executive Chair and co-founder · **Segment:** Parks/sports venue · **Updated:** 2026-10-21

## Verdict
**Moderate, real whale.** Unlike the fairgrounds, it has real turf: 5 natural grass fields on a 54-acre campus (the 7-8 outdoor and 2 indoor artificial fields are irrelevant to moles). More than 1.3 million visits a year, 30+ youth tournaments, and a soccer-community profile that reaches Reign and Seawolves people and the Sounders' history here. Five grass fields is a small turf footprint, so the account is modest. The reason to pursue it is the sports network. Check the grass fields for mounds on a walk first.

## The person
**Eric Olmstead** became President and CEO in July 2024 after almost ten years on the board. Founding partner of the Bellevue office of Washington Partners (commercial real estate) 2000-2011. Calls himself a "soccer wonk." Real-estate and board background, so he thinks in assets and long-term value. Older data sites still list Ben Oliver as CEO (stale).
**Antonio Alfaro** appears as Director of Facilities in a third-party directory. Unconfirmed; he would own field upkeep and may be the better practical contact.
**Chris Slatt** is Executive Chair and co-founder (2003) with Steve Beck and Mark Bickham.
No public posts or personal details found. Common ground: local owner-operators, youth sports.

## The organization
501(c)(3) nonprofit at 14800 Starfire Way, Tukwila, WA 98188 (King County, our area). It took operating responsibility in March 2003 under a 40-year public-private concession on the former Fort Dent Park; sources conflict on whether the landlord is King County or the City of Tukwila (unverified). Starfire runs the complex. The Sounders had offices and training here 2008-2024 and moved to Longacres in Renton; Tacoma Defiance plays here. Starfire Commons, a roughly $6M grant-funded building, is planned. As a nonprofit with a board, vendor buying is probably informal (unverified).

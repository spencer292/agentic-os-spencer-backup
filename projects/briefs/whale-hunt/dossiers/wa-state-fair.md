# Washington State Fair Events Center — Whale Dossier

**Primary:** Marty Mattes, Chief Operations Officer · **Other contacts:** Renee McClain, CEO; Alan Baker, Manager, Facilities Grounds (ZoomInfo, unverified); Andrea Thayer, Chief Experience Officer · **Segment:** Parks/sports venue · **Updated:** 2026-10-21

## Verdict
**Weak as a mole account, decent as a relationship.** The grounds are mostly buildings, asphalt, gravel and carnival/parking space. The fair publishes nothing about maintained lawn, and I found no turf acreage figure (unverified). Expect pockets of lawn and landscaping, not fine turf. The value is the network: about 150 events a year, a private-nonprofit board of local business people, and a Pierce County profile. Treat it as a "know them" whale, not a revenue whale. Spencer should confirm on a walk of the grounds before spending real effort.

## The person
**Marty Mattes** is listed as COO on thefair.com's executive team. Tenure, background and public posts: nothing found (unverified). Operations would own facilities and grounds, so he is the likely decision-maker for any grounds vendor. The title is confirmed on the fair's site; his scope is inferred.
**Renee McClain** has been CEO since October 2022 after 26 years as CFO, a lifer who knows every vendor. She succeeded Kent Hojem.
**Alan Baker** appears in a ZoomInfo directory as Manager, Facilities Grounds. Third-party data, unconfirmed, and may be outdated.
**Andrea Thayer** is Chief Experience Officer and a Washington State Fairs association director (2018-2026).
Common ground: McClain is a long-tenured local operator; Spencer is an owner-operator, company founded 2017. The Army veteran angle is untested here.

## The organization
Private nonprofit; board elected by about 1,100 shareholders (job posting; current status unverified). 110 9th Ave SW, Puyallup, WA 98371; admin phone (253) 845-1771. About 160-175 acres (sources differ), 55 full-time staff per a directory (unverified). Year-round rentals plus the September fair and a spring fair. Pierce County, inside our area. Vendor process unknown; a public-bid requirement is unlikely for a private nonprofit, but unverified. Job postings show grounds upkeep is done in-house.

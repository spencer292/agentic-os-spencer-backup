## The organization
Parks Tacoma is an independent taxing park district: five commissioners elected city-wide. Covers Tacoma plus Browns Point and Dash Point. Includes Point Defiance, Wright Park, Titlow, Northwest Trek (Eatonville), the zoo and aquarium, and Meadow Park Golf Course (27 holes, run in-house with its own director of golf and superintendent).

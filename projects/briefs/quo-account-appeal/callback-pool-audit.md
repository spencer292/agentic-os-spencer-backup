## What a carrier fraud model sees

From the outside, with no visibility into CallRail or Jobber, the Quo line looked like this:

- a phone number `(253) 683-7555` **8 days old**
- **one seat**, signing in from **Pakistan**
- dialling numbers that had **never called that number** (23 of 75 had no inbound record at all)
- including **20 numbers outside the business's own state**, some international
- with the low answer rates and short durations that returning a cold lead always produces
- **no A2P/10DLC registration**, no Free Caller Registry entry, no CNAM branding

That is the signature of a boiler room. It is also exactly what a legitimate business looks like when its lead feed is unfiltered and its outbound number is brand new.

## Remediation

1. **Filter the lead feed by area code before it reaches the receptionist.** Only 206, 253, 360, 425, 509, 564 should generate a callback task; everything else goes to a review queue for a human to look at.
2. **Never dial an international number from the business line.** No Got Moles customer has a non-US number.
3. **Set outbound caller ID to the number the customer contacted** so the callback shows a recognisable Washington number.
4. **Register the outbound number** — Free Caller Registry, CNAM branding, and A2P/10DLC before any SMS.
5. **Warm the number** — no new line goes from zero to full callback volume inside a week.

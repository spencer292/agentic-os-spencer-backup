## 2. The math

Recurring revenue compounds, so a dollar of MRR added in February is worth 11 months of billing while a dollar added in October is worth 3. Selling more in winter and spring matters far more than selling more in September.

Stacked scenarios, modeled month by month on 2026's actual add pattern:

| Scenario | 2027 revenue | Dec 2027 MRR |
|---|---:|---:|
| A. Repeat 2026's sales pace, change nothing | **$1.71M** | $144K |
| B. + price reset (§3, move 1) | $1.81M | $153K |
| C. + commercial, 1.5 new accounts/mo | $1.87M | $163K |
| D. + Quick Fix → TMCP winback, Jan–Mar | $1.94M | $169K |
| **E. + residential TMCP sales 25% above 2026** | **$2.05M** | **$182K** |
| F. + sales 40% above 2026 | $2.11M | $190K |

**Plan of record = E.** The book's own momentum gets to ~$1.7M. Price, commercial and the winback add ~$230K. The last ~$100K comes from selling 25% more TMCP than 2026.

**Correction to A:** 2026's net adds came off a smaller book. At ~$120K average MRR, 1.14% churn costs about twice what it did in 2026, roughly −$45K over the year. So A is closer to $1.66M, and the 25% sales lift in E is the minimum needed, not padding.

## 3. The six moves

### Move 1: Price reset (+~$100K in 2027) · decision needed
- **Jan 1:** 129 grandfathered TMCP jobs under $100 → $100. List is built (`cfo/2026-09-29_tmcp-price-increase-list.md`). +$1,683/mo. `KNOWN`
- **Mar 1 (notice Feb 1):** +$10/mo on the ~800 TMCP jobs at $100 and above. +~$8K/mo. `ESTIMATE`
- **Jan 1:** new-sale price list up 10% (TMCP $110/$135/$165; Quick Fix $495/$550/$660).
- Why it's sound: margin is ~29% at $100/mo against ~$35 fully loaded cost per visit (`ESTIMATE`). Visits per job stay flat at ~24/yr whatever the price, so the whole increase is margin. With 340 five-star reviews, the brand can carry a price above the market.
- Risk: if the increase causes 3% extra churn (~24 jobs, −$2.6K/mo), the net gain is still about +$5.4K/mo.

### Move 2: Quick Fix → TMCP winback, Jan–Mar (+~$65K)
- 357 repeat Quick Fix customers with a job in the last 24 months have never been offered the program (`KNOWN`, F-09).
- A winter call and text campaign offering a fixed-price spring start. Target: 15% convert, ~54 TMCP.
- Why it's sound: winter capacity is spare (5 techs on the roster, ~4 needed), and every conversion booked in Q1 bills 10–11 months.

### Move 3: Commercial, 18 new accounts in 2027 (+~$60K in-year, ~$130K/yr run-rate)
- The base today is 19 accounts at $11K/mo, ~$580 average (`KNOWN`). These are the highest-margin jobs on the board (86% margin at $200+, `ESTIMATE`).
- Channels: the LinkedIn nurture lane (golf is out-converting HOA), city and park RFPs (Renton, Burien and Marymoor already buy), and the Prologis-style industrial portfolio as one sale for many sites.
- Owner: Spencer sells, Cory bids. Target 1.5 signed per month from February.

### Move 4: Lead engine +30%, front-loaded
- Conversion is already strong: 69% quote close, 37% new-lead booking rate in July (`KNOWN`). The constraint is lead volume.
- **Before Jan 15:** attribution fixed at intake (F-01), with lead source required on every new client. Without it, extra ad spend can't be judged.
- **Feb–Apr:** move budget earlier. Ad spend goes from ~$5.2K/mo to ~$8–9K/mo Feb–Sep, but only in channels that clear a cost-per-booked-job ceiling (set with `acc-cfo`).
- Local Services Ads: confirm they're live and classified under rodent (F-11). `UNKNOWN`
- Review velocity: 340 five-star reviews at ~7 a week is the cheapest moat in the market. Keep replying to every one.

### Move 5: TMCP close rate 62% → 70%
- TMCP carries 74% of annualized booked value but has the weakest close rate (`KNOWN`).
- Use phone role-play drills on the TMCP pitch, an every-call quote rule (quoting discipline is what doubled booking in July), and a 48-hour follow-up on open TMCP quotes with dead quotes archived every week.
- Worth: 8 points on ~550 TMCP quotes/yr is ~44 extra programs.

### Move 6: Capacity in the field before the peak, not after
- Plan E means ~1,450 TMCP jobs by September 2027, ~4,500 visits/mo at peak, which is **9 techs** at 25 visits per tech-day (`ESTIMATE`). The roster is 5.
- **Hire 4:** two in the field by May 1 (post Feb 15, hire by Mar 31), one by Jun 15, one by Aug 1. A new tech needs ~15 field days to reach pace (`KNOWN`).
- Cory moves to field supervisor/GM by June. At 9 techs, hiring, training and quality are a full-time job, and a full route plus a GM role don't fit in one week.
- Routing has to hold at 9 techs. Today 13% of visits are off-tech and 29% off-day (`KNOWN`, 30-day audit). Close out the route-engine decisions before the May hires, or the extra techs add drive time instead of revenue.
- Retention is the hidden lever. Every 0.25 points of monthly churn is about ±$25K in 2027. Slipping visits and missed weekly follow-ups after a catch cause churn, so the field cadence is a revenue problem as well as an operations problem.

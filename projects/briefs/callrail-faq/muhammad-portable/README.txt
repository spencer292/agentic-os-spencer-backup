3. HOW A TRAINING SESSION WORKS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The coach asks which mode. Ask for these by name:

  DRILLS - short, high volume, no full call
    "block" ...... the 5 beats until they're automatic.  START HERE.
                   Run it until you pass twice.
    "qualify" .... stops you quoting before you've asked everything
    "pricing" .... both options, all sizes, with the guarantee, 10 in a row
    "objections" . rapid-fire, one line each
    "drill" ...... the 20 rapid-fire phone questions
    "facts" ...... mole biology, myths, mole vs vole vs gopher

  FULL CALLS
    "scenario" ... one full call from the 23 in the pack
    "shift" ...... 3 calls back to back
    "exam" ....... 5 calls, curveballs stacked, no hints, formal scorecard

The phone "rings" and the coach plays a caller. Answer exactly as you
would on the real line. Say "pause" any time to stop a call.

After each call you get a score out of 100 and coaching. 80+ is a pass.
A call with no 5-beat block cannot score above 79 no matter how well
everything else went - that is deliberate.

AT THE END OF EVERY SESSION the coach prints a scorecard block.
COPY IT AND SEND IT TO SPENCER. That is how progress gets tracked.

4. THE SERVICE DAY LOOKUP - use this on every call
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Bookmark this and keep it open all day:

     https://claude.ai/code/artifact/20e073bc-db4d-4f9c-b49e-9adc55725fba

   Sign in to claude.ai with the account Spencer gave you. The page is
   private - if it says "page not found" you are signed in with the wrong
   account. Tell Spencer; do not sign up for a new one.

   No internet? address-day-lookup.html in this folder is the same tool
   as a file. Double-click it. It never updates itself, so prefer the link.

TYPE THE CUSTOMER'S STREET ADDRESS. Two different answers come back:

  ALREADY OUR CUSTOMER -> their real next visit date, e.g. "Thursday,
  August 6", plus the visits after that. This is the answer to "when are
  you next coming?" - you no longer have to go digging in Jobber.

  NOT ON FILE -> the route day for their zip, e.g. "Tuesdays". That's the
  new-customer answer: the day we're in their area.

Type it however they say it - "15706 SE 376th" finds "15706 Southeast
376th Street". The house number alone is usually enough. A zip, a city or
a customer name works too.

ASK FOR THE ADDRESS BEFORE YOU QUOTE A DAY. Do not go off the city name.
16 of the 78 cities we serve are split across different route days
(Tacoma is on three). The lookup tells you when a city is split.

THE BIG DATE IS WHERE A TRUCK IS ACTUALLY ROUTED - not just what was
booked. Jobber holds what was booked; OptimoRoute holds where the trucks
are really going. The lookup shows OptimoRoute's answer once routes are
planned. Give that date.

WATCH FOR THE COLORED BOXES
  RED "The two systems disagree" -> give the ROUTED date on the card.
      Then tell Spencer - the customer's email and text went off the
      other date and they are expecting the wrong day.
  RED "Not on the planned route" -> booked, but no truck is going. DO NOT
      confirm it. Say you'll double-check and call back today, tell Spencer.
  AMBER "falls on a Tuesday, but the zip normally runs Mondays" -> give
      them the date on the card, it's what's scheduled. Tell Spencer after.
  AMBER "No visit currently scheduled" -> on file, nothing booked. Book on
      the zip's route day shown right below it.
  "Routes for that week are not planned yet" -> normal for anything past
      this Friday. The date is fine to give.

IT IS A SNAPSHOT, NOT LIVE. Rebuilt every morning at 6:45, so something
booked at 10am shows up tomorrow, not today. Check the build time in the
footer. Using the link? Just reload - if the footer still isn't today's
date, tell Spencer. Using the file? It is frozen at the day it was sent
to you; ask Spencer for a fresh one.

What to SAY about scheduling is in 2026-08-03_service-day-scripts.md.

5. JOBBER - CREATING CLIENTS AND SENDING QUOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full step-by-step: 2026-08-05_muhammad-jobber-training.pdf. Everything in
it - line-item names, quantities, deposits - is copied from how the real
quotes in the account are actually built.

THE FIVE THINGS THAT WILL BITE YOU IF YOU SKIP THE DOC

  1. SEARCH BEFORE YOU CREATE. Name, then phone, then street address.
     Three searches, ten seconds. The account already has 114 duplicate
     records and manual office entry is a top cause. A phone number with
     no name is a CallRail stub - FILL IT IN, don't make a new one.

  2. TMCP QUANTITY IS 12, NOT 1. Twelve months at the monthly rate. If
     your TMCP quote totals $100, you left the quantity at 1.

  3. AFTER ADDING A DISCOUNT, LOOK AT THE TOTAL. A discount only takes
     money off if its unit price is NEGATIVE. If the total went UP, the
     minus sign is missing. (Military and First Responder were both saved
     wrong in the account - fixed 2026-08-05 - but check anyway.)
     Friends-and-family at $250 is NOT a discount line, it's a
     replacement price, and it stays positive.

  4. DEPOSIT: $150 on the Quick Fix, $0 on TMCP.

  5. CHECK THE EMAIL ADDRESS. A typo means the quote goes nowhere and
     everyone assumes the customer ghosted us.

COMMERCIAL OF ANY SIZE, OR RESIDENTIAL OVER 5 ACRES -> DO NOT QUOTE.
Those are in-person bids and they go to CORY.

WHO SCHEDULES WHAT
  Existing customer - next visit, reschedule, nothing booked?
      STILL YOU, off the address lookup. Unchanged.
  Brand-new customer whose quote just got approved?
      CORY. Tag the client 'Schedule requested' and tell him.

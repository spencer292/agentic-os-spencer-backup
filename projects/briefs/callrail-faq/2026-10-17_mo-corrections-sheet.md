# Mo — Corrections Sheet (Sep 17 – Oct 16 calls)

I went through your calls from the last month. A lot of it is really good: your quotes go out in minutes with everything right, your service days are almost always correct, and customers like talking to you. Your best calls (Quaschnik, Lanz, Boerner, Peters) were as good as anyone's.

These are the six fixes that matter most. Each one shows what you said on a real call and what to say instead.

## 1. The year-round plan goes FIRST, with your recommendation

This is the big one. On the last month of calls, the year-round plan went first only 3 times.

**What happened:** Souvik asked what you'd recommend, and you said *"Yeah, yeah, that. That is also a good option"* about the $450. Eugene said *"Sign me up for a year"* and ended up on the Quick Fix.

**Say this instead (after the 5 beats):**

> "There are two ways to do this, and I'll tell you which one I'd put you on. Most people in your situation take the **year-round program**: $100 a month, no deposit, unlimited visits. We catch the ones you've got now, then we keep the perimeter covered so the next one gets caught before it does damage.
> There's also a **one-month option**: $450, five weekly visits, $150 up front, and if we catch nothing, the $150 is all you pay. That clears what's there today. It doesn't stop the next one.
> For a yard that's had them [however long they said], **I'd take the year-round one.**"

**If they say they want the year-round plan, stop selling and book it.**

## 2. Teach before you price

None of the 92 calls got all 5 beats in before the price.

**When they ask the price first, say:**

> "I'll give you both prices in about a minute. Let me get two quick things so I quote you right, and I'll tell you what you're actually dealing with."

Then ask the zip, the acreage and how long they've had them. Then give the 5 beats: the food, the net, the dinner bell, the new hills, and territorial ("not if, but when"). **Then** give the price.

## 3. "Let me talk to my spouse" means get the info first

At least 15 warm callers hung up with no name, email or address. Without those, we can't follow up, and the lead is gone.

**Say this:**

> "Totally makes sense. Let me get your name and email, and I'll send it over so you two can look at it together. No commitment."

Then get the name, cell, email and street address, one at a time, and read the address back.

## 4. Only say what's true and on the sheet

Customers trust you because you're honest. Don't trade that for a big claim.

## 5. Price by the PROPERTY size, not the lawn

JT Wilcox (1.5 acres), Julie Penrod (3 acres), Chad Kuipers (1.2 acres) and Lester Boerner (5 acres) were all quoted the under-1-acre price because the lawn was small.

**The tier is the whole property:**

- under 1 acre: $450 / $100
- 1–3 acres: $500 / $125
- 3–5 acres: $600 / $150
- **over 5 acres or any commercial/HOA property:** no phone price. Take their info and pass it to **Cory** for an in-person bid.

If they don't know the size, use the higher tier or look it up. Never pick the cheaper one and add a disclaimer.

# THE 5-BEAT OPENER — say this before you say a price

---

## FIRST, THREE QUESTIONS

**1.** "Where are you located?"  **2.** "Is it over or under an acre?"  **3.** "How long have you been dealing with the moles?"

*(Residential or commercial? — ask if it isn't obvious.)*

---

## THEN, THE 5 BEATS — straight through, no stopping

### 1 · THE FOOD
> "So moles eat worms. And they have to eat about **70% of their body weight every single day** just to stay alive."

### 2 · THE NET
> "The way their tunnel system works is **like a net to catch that food**. The worms fall into the tunnel, the mole runs along it and eats them."

### 3 · THE DINNER BELL
> "This time of year everything around you is drying out — so your watered lawn is where all the worms are. **You've basically rung the dinner bell.**"

### 4 · THE NEW HILLS
> "When a tunnel stops producing enough worms, the mole has to dig new tunnel to catch more. **And that dirt has to go somewhere. That's the new hills you're seeing.**"

### 5 · TERRITORIAL → NOT IF, BUT WHEN
> "And moles are **territorial**. So it's **one or two moles** doing all that damage, not a hundred. But that also means **when we catch this one, the next one moves into the empty territory.** So there's no such thing as one-and-done mole control — **it's not a question of if they come back, it's when, and how often.**"

---

## → "SO THERE ARE TWO WAYS TO GO ABOUT THIS."

### **Now** the prices.

**Quick Fix** — one month, weekly visits, **$450** (under an acre). $150 up front. **Catch nothing and the $150 is all you pay.**
**Total Mole Control** — year round, unlimited visits, **$100/month** (under an acre). Nothing up front, billed the last day of the month. 12-month term.

# No number leaves your mouth until all 5 beats are done.

## IF THEY SAY "I'LL JUST DO THE ONE MONTH AND SEE"

> "Totally fair — a lot of people start there. Only thing I'd say is the one month proves we can catch them, but it doesn't stop the next one moving in, because that territory opens right back up. **Four calls a year is $1,800. The program is $1,200 and we never leave.**"

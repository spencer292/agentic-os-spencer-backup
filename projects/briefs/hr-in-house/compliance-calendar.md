# HR Compliance Calendar — Got Moles

Recurring obligations with an owner and a date. A retainer is often really a subscription to
somebody else remembering these. Once they are written down with dates, that value largely
disappears.

Owner is Spencer unless noted. "Payroll" means the payroll provider handles it — but confirm each
one in writing once, and file the confirmation, because an obligation you assume is handled is an
obligation nobody is doing.

---

## Every pay period
| Item | Owner |
|---|---|
| Hours reviewed for overtime, including anyone salaried below the exempt threshold | Spencer |
| PFML and WA Cares withheld at the current rate | Payroll |

## Monthly
| Item | Owner |
|---|---|

## Annually — January, the heavy month
| Item | Owner |
|---|---|
| **Update `wa-compliance.md`**: minimum wage, exempt salary threshold, PFML rate, WA Cares rate, non-compete threshold, local city minimums | ops-hr |
| **Local minimum HEADROOM check** — not just a rate refresh. Compare the *lowest field wage* against the *highest local minimum in territory*. At 2026 close: $22.50 vs Tukwila $21.65, headroom $0.85. These reindex ~4%/yr, so Tukwila lands near $22.52 in 2027 and the margin plausibly goes negative without anyone touching a wage | Spencer |
| Renton mid-tier steps to $21.57 on **2026-07-01** — mid-year, not January. Do not assume all local movement happens in the January sweep | ops-hr |
| **Recheck every salaried employee against the new exempt threshold.** It rises to 2.5× minimum wage by 2028 — people cross the line without a pay cut | Spencer |
| Replace all workplace posters with the new-year versions. Free from L&I and ESD — never pay a poster vendor | Spencer |
| Reissue the paid sick leave policy if it changed | Spencer |
| Confirm the L&I risk classification still matches the work actually performed | Spencer |

## Event-driven — the clock starts on the event, not on the paperwork
| Trigger | Deadline |
|---|---|
| New hire | I-9 Section 1 by day one; Section 2 within **3 business days**; WA new-hire report within **20 days** |
| Day 90 of employment | Paid sick leave becomes usable; competency gate; first review |
| Separation | Final pay by the end of the next established pay period; access removed same day; memo written same day |
| Workplace fatality or hospitalisation | **Report to L&I within 8 hours** |
| Personnel file request from an employee or former employee | Generally **21 calendar days** |
| Injury on the job | L&I claim filed; medical documents to `hr-private/medical/`, never the personnel file |
| Headcount reaches 8 | WLAD applies — handbook and complaint procedure reviewed by an attorney |
| Headcount reaches 15 | Title VII, ADA, and **mandatory pay range disclosure in job postings** |
| Headcount reaches 50 | FMLA, ACA employer mandate, PFML employer premium share, PFML job restoration |

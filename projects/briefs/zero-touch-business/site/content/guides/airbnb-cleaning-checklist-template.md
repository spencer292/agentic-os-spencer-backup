An Airbnb cleaning checklist has to do two jobs a standard clean doesn't: reset the space to guest-ready condition (staged, restocked, damage-checked) and do it inside a tight turnover window, often same-day between checkout and the next check-in. It's closer to a hybrid of a standard clean and a hospitality inspection than a regular recurring visit. Below is a full room-by-room template, how turnover pricing differs from standard rates, and how to structure the job so a tight window doesn't turn into a missed check-in.

## What makes an Airbnb turnover clean different from a standard clean?

The turnaround time and the staging requirement. A standard recurring clean maintains a home someone already lives in; a turnover clean resets a space between two sets of strangers, usually on a same-day deadline set by checkout and check-in times, not by your crew's schedule. On top of the normal clean, you're restocking consumables, re-staging furniture and linens to match listing photos, and checking for guest damage or missing items before the next booking arrives.

That combination of a hard deadline, staging, and an inspection is why turnover cleans get quoted and scheduled differently from a recurring residential clean. Hosts pay a premium for a crew that's reliable on same-day timing specifically.

## What should an Airbnb cleaning checklist include, room by room?

Everything on a standard-clean list, plus staging, restocking, and a damage check built into every room instead of treated as an afterthought.

**Kitchen**
- Dishes washed, dried, and put away (never left in the sink or dishwasher)
- Countertops, stovetop, and sink cleaned and dried
- Fridge wiped down and checked for guest-left food
- Trash and recycling emptied, new liners placed
- Consumables restocked (coffee, dish soap, paper towels) per host's par level

**Bathrooms**
- Toilet, tub/shower, and sink scrubbed and dried
- Mirrors streak-free
- Towels replaced with fresh sets, folded to the host's staging standard
- Toiletries restocked (soap, toilet paper, shampoo if provided)
- Trash emptied, new liner placed

**Bedrooms and living areas**
- Beds fully stripped and remade with fresh linens (never spot-fixed)
- Under-bed and behind-furniture check for guest-left items
- Surfaces dusted, remotes and light switches wiped down
- Furniture and decor re-staged to match listing photos
- Blinds/curtains reset to the host's preferred position

**Whole-property / turnover-specific**
- Walkthrough for damage or missing items, logged with photos
- Guest manual, keys, or lockbox codes reset per host instructions
- Trash taken to the curb or bin, not left staged inside
- Thermostat and lights reset to host's standard settings
- Final photo walkthrough before leaving, timestamped

## How do you price an Airbnb turnover clean versus a standard clean?

By treating the turnover premium as compensation for speed and reliability, not just for the extra scope. A crew that can guarantee a 2-3 hour same-day window between a checkout and a check-in is worth more to a host than one offering the same checklist on a flexible schedule, because a missed turnover means a guest showing up to an unready unit.

| Unit size | Standard clean (typical) | Airbnb turnover (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Studio / 1 bed | $100–$150 | $90–$140 |
| 2 bed / 1-2 bath | $150–$220 | $130–$200 |
| 3 bed / 2 bath | $200–$280 | $180–$260 |
| 4+ bed / 2+ bath | $280–$400+ | $260–$380+ |

Turnover pricing often lands slightly under a comparable standard clean per visit because the scope, while broader in staging, is faster overall on a well-maintained short-term rental. The real money is in volume and recurring cadence, not any single visit. These are US suburban starting ranges and vary by market, unit condition, and how far the property sits from your route.

## How do you build turnover cleaning into a route without blowing deadlines?

By treating checkout and check-in times as hard scheduling constraints, not soft ones, and building buffer into every route rather than stacking turnovers back to back. A single turnover running long doesn't just cost you time. It risks a guest arriving to a unit that isn't ready, which is the fastest way to lose a host relationship built on same-day reliability.

Hosts with multiple units are also some of the best recurring accounts in a cleaning business, since one relationship can mean several turnovers a week on a predictable [recurring schedule](/guides/cleaning-business-startup-checklist/). Worth prioritizing route density here the same way you would for [any recurring account](/guides/route-density-service-business/): cluster same-market turnovers on the days that already make sense for your route.

## Checklist before you send the quote

- [ ] Confirmed checkout and next check-in times before quoting the turnaround
- [ ] Priced off the turnover range, not a flat standard-clean rate
- [ ] Restocking par levels agreed with the host in writing
- [ ] Photo walkthrough required on every visit, not just when something's wrong
- [ ] Route buffer built in: no turnover scheduled back-to-back with zero slack

## FAQ

**Do I need to photograph every turnover, or only when there's damage?**
Every turnover. A timestamped photo walkthrough protects your crew if a host disputes the unit's condition later, and it takes under two minutes once it's built into the routine.

**Who pays for restocked consumables, the host or the cleaning business?**
The host, almost always. Agree on a per-visit restocking budget or have the host supply consumables in bulk; either way, put it in writing so it isn't renegotiated after the fact.

**Should Airbnb turnovers go on the same [price list](/guides/cleaning-service-price-list-template/) as standard cleans?**
List it as its own service line. The scope (staging, restocking, damage checks) and the scheduling constraints (same-day turnaround) are different enough that blending it into "standard clean" pricing confuses hosts and under-prices the reliability you're actually selling.

**What if the guest leaves the unit in much worse shape than usual?**
Note it on the [quote](/guides/cleaning-quote-template/) process up front: a re-quote or trip-charge clause for turnovers that run well beyond the standard scope, communicated to the host before you start, not after the invoice goes out.

**Is same-day turnover cleaning worth specializing in?**
For a route-based cleaning business, yes. A host with several units can become one of your most reliable recurring accounts, and same-day reliability is a real differentiator most solo cleaners can't offer at scale.

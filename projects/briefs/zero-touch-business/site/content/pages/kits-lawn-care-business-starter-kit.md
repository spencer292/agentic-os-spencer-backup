## FAQ

**Can I edit the templates?**
Yes — every file is fully editable. Replace the placeholders with your business name, adjust the terms and the rates, and use them as your own.

**Are these legal documents?**
No. They're business templates written from operating experience, and they're a far better starting point than a blank page. Contract law varies by state, so have a local attorney review anything you'll rely on — particularly the waiver.

**Will the pricing calculator work in my market?**
The defaults are sane 2026 US suburban starting points, and every rate is a yellow cell you can change: target hourly rate, mowing production rates, obstacle factors, frequency multipliers, and your mowing weeks per season. Southern operators mow forty weeks; northern operators mow twenty-six. Set yours.

**Does it handle biweekly clients?**
Yes — and it prices them *higher* per visit, not lower. Double the growth and double the mess for the same drive is a cost, not a discount, and most new operators get that backwards.

**Do I need special software?**
No. Word and Excel work, and so do the free Google and Apple equivalents.

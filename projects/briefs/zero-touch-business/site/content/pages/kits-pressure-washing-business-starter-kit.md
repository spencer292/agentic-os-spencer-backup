## FAQ

**Can I edit the templates?**
Yes — every file is fully editable. Replace the placeholders with your business name, adjust the terms and the rates, and use them as your own. That's the whole point.

**Are these legal documents?**
No. They're business templates written from operating experience, and they're a far better starting point than a blank page. Contract law varies by state, so have a local attorney review anything you'll rely on — particularly the waiver.

**Will the pricing calculator work in my market?**
The defaults are sane 2026 US suburban starting points, and every rate is a yellow cell you can change: target hourly rate, production rates by service type, condition factors, and your minimum job floor. Time yourself on your first ten jobs and put your real numbers in — that's what makes it yours.

**Do I need special software?**
No. Word and Excel work, and so do the free Google and Apple equivalents.

**Is this the same as the cleaning kit?**
No. Same operating philosophy, entirely different trade. The pricing model is built on surface area and production rates, the checklists are soft-wash and flatwork procedures, and the waiver addresses the damage claims specific to putting water on somebody's building.

## Description

You can figure out how to cut grass. What sinks new lawn care businesses is everything around the mowing: pricing a small fenced yard like an open one, mowing without an agreement, chasing payment every month, and never working out that the twenty minutes between stops is where the profit went.

This kit is the paperwork layer, done:

**Price it right**
- Pricing Calculator (Excel/Google Sheets) — enter turf area, obstacle level, service type, and frequency; get a per-visit price with trim time and unload time counted, plus monthly and season estimates and a minimum stop price you can't quote under. Biweekly clients price HIGHER per visit, not lower — most new operators get that backwards.
- Job Costing Sheet — per-visit labor, fuel, materials, and equipment reserve, so you find the long-standing clients whose price stopped working three years ago
- Income & Expense Tracker — monthly P&L with this trade's categories: fuel, blades and parts, equipment repair, fertilizer, dump fees

**Run the route**
- Recurring Client & Route Tracker — every client by service day, a route-day board showing revenue per hour for each day, and a density tab that puts a number on what drive time is costing you. Route density is the entire economics of this business, and this is the workbook that makes it visible.

**Protect the work**
- Property Condition Acknowledgment & Damage Waiver — the two calls that damage this business fastest are a cut irrigation head and a severed invisible-fence wire. This makes marking the client's job in writing, covers thrown objects and trimmer contact, and includes an addendum for when a client insists you scalp the lawn in July.
- Residential Service Agreement + Commercial Landscape Maintenance Contract
- Quote/Estimate and Invoice templates, Client Intake Form, Client Welcome Packet
- Policies & Letters — weather and scheduling policy, cancellation/skip/no-access policy, price-increase letter, review-request and complaint scripts

**Work the season**
- Seasonal Service Schedule & Checklists — the standard mowing visit, spring cleanup, fertilization and treatment (including the applicator licensing gate), aeration and overseeding, fall cleanup and winterization
- Zero-to-First-Client startup checklist — licensing, insurance, realistic 2026 equipment budgets, applicator licensing before you sell any treatment, and how to build a dense route instead of a scattered one

**Format:** Editable Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files. Open in Word and Excel, or import straight into Google Docs, Google Sheets, Pages, Numbers, or LibreOffice. Instant delivery, nothing to install.

**Who it's for:** solo operators and small crews starting or tightening up a lawn care or landscape maintenance business in the US.

**Who it's not for:** franchises with corporate paperwork, anyone looking for legal advice, and anyone wanting agronomy training — these are business templates; have a local attorney review contracts and the waiver before use.

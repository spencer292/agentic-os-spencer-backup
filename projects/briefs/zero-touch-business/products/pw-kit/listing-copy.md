## Description

You can figure out how to wash a house. What sinks new pressure washing businesses is everything around the washing: quoting by feel, working without a waiver, and getting blamed for paint that was already failing before you showed up.

This kit is the paperwork layer, done:

**Price it right**
- Pricing Calculator (Excel/Google Sheets) — enter surface area, service type, and condition; get a flat-rate quote built from real production rates, with setup and drive time counted and a minimum-job floor so you physically can't quote a money-losing job
- Job Costing Sheet — chemical, labor, fuel, and equipment reserve per job, showing your true hourly earnings instead of your quoted ones
- Income & Expense Tracker — monthly P&L with this trade's categories: chemicals, fuel, equipment repair, insurance

**Protect the work**
- Liability Waiver & Property Condition Acknowledgment — the pre-job walkthrough that documents chalking, failed paint, cracked concrete, and rotted wood BEFORE you clean, plus the waiver and a client-directed-service addendum for jobs you'd rather advise against. This is the single most important document in the trade and the one most new washers don't have.
- Residential Service Agreement + Commercial Contract & Bid — scope, access, weather rescheduling, payment terms
- Quote/Estimate and Invoice templates, Client Intake Form, Client Welcome Packet

**Run the jobs**
- Job & Client Tracker — every completed job with an automatic next-service-due date, so your best lead source becomes the customers you already have
- Policies & Letters — weather and rescheduling policy, cancellation and no-show policy, price-increase letter, review-request and complaint scripts
- Job Checklists — house wash (soft wash), concrete and flatwork, roof soft wash, decks and fences, gutter face brightening, plus pre-job and final walkthroughs
- Zero-to-First-Client startup checklist — licensing, insurance exclusions that actually matter, realistic 2026 equipment budgets, and the wastewater and runoff rules most new washers learn the expensive way

**Format:** Editable Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) files. Open in Word and Excel, or import straight into Google Docs, Google Sheets, Pages, Numbers, or LibreOffice. Instant delivery, nothing to install.

**Who it's for:** solo operators and small crews starting or tightening up a pressure washing or exterior cleaning business in the US.

**Who it's not for:** franchises with corporate paperwork, and anyone looking for legal advice — these templates are starting points; have a local attorney review contracts and the waiver before use.

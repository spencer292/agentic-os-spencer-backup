# Module 4 — How to Walk a Property

> **STATUS: NOT READY TO TRAIN FROM.** Spencer gave the walk pattern on 2026-07-31 but the
> follow-up interview was not completed. The geometry below is captured verbatim-in-substance
> from him; everything marked OPEN is unresolved. **Do not hand this to a tech yet.**

This is the biggest module in the program. It is the skill the entire job rests on — every
trap you set is only as good as the walk that chose the spot. The current Field Guide covers
it in seven bullets (§2.2), which is why techs do it differently from each other.

---

## What Spencer gave us (2026-07-31)

**The walk is a fixed, repeatable pattern — not "wander around and look."**

1. **Start by looking at the house.**
2. **Walk the outer loop** — approximately 10 ft in off the perimeter of the property, all
   the way around, until you reach the opposite side.
3. **Walk the inner loop** — clockwise, approximately 10 ft off the footing of the house.

```
┌─── property line ───────────────┐
│  ┌ ── outer loop (10 ft in) ─┐  │
│  │    ┌── inner loop ──┐     │  │
│  │    │  ┌─────────┐   │     │  │
│  │    │  │  HOUSE  │   │     │  │
│  │    │  └─────────┘   │     │  │
│  │    └ 10 ft off footing ┘  │  │
│  └───────────────────────────┘  │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### This applies to EVERY visit

**Repeat visits get the same full property evaluation, in the same sequence.** A repeat
visit is not "go check the traps." You walk the property first to find what the moles have
done since you left — new activity shows up in places your existing traps aren't. Checking
traps follows the same sequence of events.

This is a real change from how the Field Guide reads today, and it is the standard.

## OPEN — interview must be completed before this module can be written

1. **Confirm the loop geometry.** Two concentric loops as diagrammed? And does the outer
   loop go all the way around back to the start, or literally to the far side and then cut
   in to the house loop? ("...all the way around until they get to the opposite side.")
2. **Why 10 feet?** The tech needs the reason, not just the number — edge runs, sightline,
   or staying off the tunnels?
3. **"Start by looking at the house"** — what is being read in those first thirty seconds?
   Grade, landscaping, irrigation, where the ground stays wet?
4. **Does the customer walk with you?** Field Guide §4.3 pillar 4 says walk it with them and
   point things out — that is the trust-builder. Does that survive a fixed loop pattern on a
   first visit, or do you walk alone then bring them out?
5. **Probe rod — during or after?** Probing as you walk the loops, or eyes-only on the loops
   and the probe comes out once spots are picked?
6. **Large properties.** Does the two-loop pattern hold on 3–5 acres, or change past a size?
7. **Time budget.** Process A allows 30–45 min for the whole first visit. How much is the walk?
8. **What comes out of it?** Counting mounds, photos, flagging as you go — or all held in
   your head until you commit to the four trap locations? *This feeds Module 8 directly:
   the activity code (M/A / L/A / N/A) that drives follow-up cadence has to be decided
   somewhere on this walk.*

# Technician Training Program — Zero to Hero

## Goal

Take a person with zero mole-control experience and, through a structured program with
checkpoints, produce a technician who can run a full route solo to the Got Moles standard —
parking, property assessment, trapping, customer conversation, selling, and Jobber
documentation — without supervision.

## Why now

1. A tech hire is coming and there is nothing to hand them.
2. The existing crew is inconsistent (see the 2026-07-10 Jobber note audit — four different
   follow-up conventions in use, most visits logged with no note at all).
3. Hiring is the growth bottleneck. The program has to outlive any one trainer.

## Deliverables

1. **Field Guide v2** — the reference manual. Existing guide deepened and corrected.
2. **Onboarding Program** — day-by-day path from day 1 to solo-certified, with module lessons.
3. **Ride-along cards** — what the trainer watches for and signs off on, per module.
4. **Field checklists** — pocket/phone-sized: park & approach, the walk, the set, departure.
5. **Competency sign-off sheet** — the gate a tech must pass to run a route alone.
6. **Role-play drills** — customer-conversation and objection practice, phone-pack style.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new hire can be handed the program on day 1 and work through it with a trainer who is
  not Spencer.
- Every module ends in something observable — a checklist a trainer initials, not a lesson read.
- Nothing in the program is invented. Every standard traces to Spencer's stated practice or
  an existing documented rule.
- Follow-up cadence is taught by activity code (M/A, L/A, N/A), not by flat recurrence.
- Techs are taught to hand commercial and 5+ acre bids to Cory, never to quote them.

## Constraints

- US English spelling throughout.
- No I-713 compliance claims. Chemical-free / safe-for-pets-and-children messaging only.
- Trap methodology (§3) and sales methodology (§4) stay INTERNAL — never published.
- Claims guardrails: 219+ five-star reviews, 5,000 clients, 3 GBP locations. No "WA's #1".
  "15+ years" is Spencer's personal experience — company founded 2017.
- Content is captured by interviewing Spencer module by module. No guessing.

## Dependencies

- Spencer's time for the module interviews — this is the critical path.
- Confirmation of who runs ride-alongs (Cory? Cammeron?) — the program needs a trainer role.

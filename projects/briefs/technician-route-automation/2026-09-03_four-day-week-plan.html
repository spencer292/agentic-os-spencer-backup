The load fits. This is a geography problem, not a capacity problem — and nothing next week has been promised to a single customer yet, so you have a free hand right up until the day before each route runs.

Three things came off the board that change how to approach it. First, next week's real demand is about the same as this week's — 584 visits and 184 tech-hours — which over four days is 26 to 34 stops per tech per day. Every one of your five has already run a day that size in August. Second, Washington has no daily overtime; the threshold is 40 hours in the week, so four longer days cost nothing that five normal ones didn't. Third, arrival windows only go out the day before at 14:00, and the crons are stopped — so no customer next week has been told anything.

What doesn't fit is the ground. Monday isn't a fifth of the workload spread evenly. For each tech it's a specific physical block of the map: Tavis's Monday is Burien and Skyway, Alias's is the Bellevue–Kirkland–Redmond core, Luke's is Olympia north of I-5. Dumping those blocks onto Tuesday — which is what's on the board right now — means five techs each driving two separate territories in one day.

Run order

  1. Leave the crons stopped through the replanThey already are. Check route-drift-check stays active: false before anything restarts — in fix mode it reads a manual move as drift, re-sequences the whole day and writes it back underneath you.
  2. Prune stale OptimoRoute orders firstfetch-window-visits.mjs, then prune-stale-orders.mjs. Ghost stops carried 189 minutes of imaginary drive last time and made a balanced day look broken.
  3. Move the tiles in JobberDates only, per the five blocks above. Nothing on Monday, nothing on the weekend.
  4. Then apply the 42 deferrals, +7 days eachIn this order, not before — a deferred visit adds seven days to its re-cut date, so it lands on its own home weekday in the week of 9/14 instead of following the parked Tuesday. Deferring first puts Alias's Bellevue work on a Tuesday it has never run.
  5. Re-run assign-by-territory for the lendsCory taking Tavis's Skyway corner and Robert's Covington won't stick otherwise — a moved visit keeps its old assignee, which is how eleven visits landed on Tavis three days before his handover.
  6. Push to OptimoRoute, optimize each day, write times backBalancing OFF, not ON_FORCE — force reassigns across drivers and would undo step 4.
  7. Let the arrival-window sweep runEvery job needs its 3-hour window before the D-1 14:00 notification cutoff, or the customer gets an exact route ETA instead of a window.
  8. Re-check Sunday 9/6Another ~110 visits will have landed by then. Re-run the day balance against the filled board, not against today's.
